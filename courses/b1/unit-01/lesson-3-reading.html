📖 Unit 1 · Reading (exam practice)

💡 Exam tips

  1. Read the title and look at the text quickly.
  2. Read each question. Then find the part of the text that answers it.
  3. Check all the options (a, b, c). Wrong options often use words from the text, but they say something different.

📄 A new hobby

Last year, Marta, a 28-year-old office worker from Cēsis, felt tired and stressed. She worked long hours and spent most evenings on the sofa looking at her phone. "I had no energy and no hobbies," she says.

Then a colleague invited her to a Saturday morning running group. Marta did not want to go at first, but she went and met a friendly group of people. They run five kilometres, and afterwards they have coffee together. "I couldn't run for five minutes at the start," Marta says. "Now I can run for forty minutes without stopping."

Marta has changed her routine. She now goes to bed earlier, and she doesn't stay up late on her phone. She has also made three new friends. This year she has already run in two city races, and she is training for a half marathon in October. "Running is not only about being fit," she says. "It makes me feel happy. I wish I had started earlier."
