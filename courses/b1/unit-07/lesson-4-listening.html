Unit 7 · Listening (exam practice)

Exam tips: 1) Read the questions before you listen. 2) You hear the recording twice. 3) Listen for the answer, not for every word. 4) Write an answer for every question.

Show the script (read it after you finish)

Newsreader: Good morning. Here are the headlines. The government announced that it would reduce taxes on electric cars next year. The minister said that this would help to reduce air pollution.
In sport, the Latvian basketball team won last night's match against Estonia, seventy-eight to seventy-one. The coach said that he was very proud of his players.
In local news, a fire broke out in a shopping centre in Daugavpils yesterday afternoon. Nobody was hurt, but about two hundred people had to leave the building. Firefighters said that the fire had started in a restaurant kitchen.
And finally, the weather. The forecast says it will be cloudy with some rain in the morning, but the afternoon will be sunny and warm, with temperatures up to twenty-two degrees. The weekend will be warmer, won't it? Well, that depends on where you live, but the east of the country will see the sun on Saturday.
That's all for now. The next news will be at noon.
