## A. Ziņotie uzdevumi

### 1. ppc-B11-5a-r2 · Present perfect continuous · B1.1 · izvēle
- **Bija:** Your friend is out of breath because she was running until a minute ago. Which sentence explains why? → *I've been running.*
- **Problēma:** jautājums ir par draudzeni, bet atbilde ir pirmajā personā.
- **Jaunais:** Your friend arrives out of breath. You ask why. What does she say?
- ✔ I've been running. ✘ I'm going to run. · I run every day. · I ran last year.
- Skaidrojumi nemainās.

### 2. var-pp-b11-match-01 · Present perfect · B1.1 · savienošana ("too easy")
- **Ieteikums: izņemt.** Labās puses ir teorijas uzlīmes (*The length of the period*, *The starting point*), tāpēc uzdevums ir par viegli un neko nemāca. Tas nav tas pats saturs kā B.1 (šeit ir *for / since / yet*); ja gribi *for / since* savienošanu paturēt, uzrakstīšu jaunu ar teikumiem, nevis uzlīmēm.

### 4. c-3118 · Narrative tenses · B2.1 · ierakstāma forma
- **Bija:** She ___ in Madrid very long, but she knew the city well. (not / live)
- **Problēma:** arī *didn't live* ir pareiza angļu valoda.
- **Jaunais:** When I first met her, she ___ in Madrid very long, but she already knew the city well. (not / live)
- ✔ hadn't been living · had not been living · hadn't lived · had not lived
- Skaidrojums nemainās. Ar *When I first met her* un *already* darbībai jābūt pirms cita pagātnes brīža, tāpēc *didn't live* vairs neder.

### 5. fu-B11-5a-r3 · Future · B1.1 · izvēle
- **Bija:** Your friend says: 'I will meet Anna at 6, we agreed yesterday.' Choose the more natural option for an arrangement.
- **Problēma:** komats starp diviem teikumiem; nepareizie varianti ir neiespējami (*I meet Anna at 6 yesterday*).
- **Jaunais:** Yesterday you and Anna agreed to meet at 6 today. At lunch a colleague asks about your plans for this evening. What do you say?

- *At lunch … plans for this evening* nosaka, ka runa ir par nākotni (otrā lasītāja atradums: bez tā *I met Anna at 6* būtu pareizi nākamajā rītā). Nepareizie varianti ir īstas formas, kas nerunā par nākotni. *I'll meet* un *I meet* apzināti nav doti, jo par tiem var strīdēties.
- Skaidrojumi nemainās.

### 7. c-0037 · Modal verbs · B1.1 · vārdu secība

## B. Savienošanas uzdevumi

Visi ir mācību uzdevumi (neskaitās pierādījumos). Pārbaude katram: samainot jebkurus divus galus, teikums vairs nav jēdzīgs.

### B.1. Present perfect · B1.1 (b1-tenses-2, 26. rinda)
- **Bija:** Match each sentence with its meaning. (*a result now / something that continues / life experience / a very recent action*)
- **Jaunais:** Match each sentence with what it tells us.
  - I've lost my keys. = I can't open the door now.
  - I've lived here for ten years. = I moved here ten years ago and I'm still here.
  - I've been to Italy twice. = I have visited it two times in my life.
  - I've just finished. = The work was done a minute ago.
- EN: The present perfect links the past with now: a result, a time up to now, an experience or a very recent action.
- LV: Present Perfect saista pagātni ar tagadni: rezultāts, laiks līdz šim brīdim, pieredze vai nupat notikusi darbība.

### B.2. Present continuous · B1.1 (b1-tenses-1, 67. rinda)
- **Bija:** Match each sentence with its meaning. (*an action now / a temporary situation / a change / an arrangement*)
- **Jaunais:** Match each beginning with its ending.
  - I'm waiting for a call, = so I can't switch my phone off.
  - She's staying with us this week, = until her new flat is ready.
  - It's getting darker, = so let's turn the lights on.
  - We're flying to Rome on Friday, = and the tickets are already booked.
- EN: The present continuous: an action now, a temporary situation, a change, or a fixed arrangement.
- LV: Present Continuous: darbība tagad, pagaidu situācija, pārmaiņa vai norunāts plāns.

### B.3. Future · A2.2 (a2-big, 124. rinda)
- **Bija:** Match each sentence with its meaning. (*an offer / a plan / a prediction from what I see / an arrangement*)
- **Jaunais:** Match each situation with the sentence that follows.
  - That bag looks heavy. = I'll carry it for you.
  - Look at those black clouds. = It's going to rain.
  - I bought a French book yesterday. = I'm going to learn French.
  - I have a toothache. = I'm seeing the dentist at 4.
- EN: *will* for an offer made now; *going to* for a plan or for what we can see is coming; the present continuous for an arrangement with a time.
- LV: *will*: piedāvājums, ko izsaka šajā brīdī; *going to*: nodoms vai tas, ko redzam tuvojamies; Present Continuous: norunāta lieta ar laiku.

### B.4. Izņemt (Inga piekrita 11. novembrī)
- Present continuous passive · B1.2 (b1-passives, 106. rinda): *The bridge is being repaired / is repaired every spring / has been repaired / was repaired*.
- Future passive · B1.2 (b1-passives, 172. rinda): *It will be done / has been done / was done / is being done*.
- Rindas tiek izņemtas no faila. Serverī tās jāizņem ar *Retire* sarakstā *Approved*: imports pats neizņem uzdevumu, kura failā vairs nav. Pētījuma datiem ietekmes nav (mācību uzdevumi, atbildes saglabājas).

## E. Atstāts tavam lēmumam

- **Izlemts (Inga, 11. novembris): atstāstījumā pieturamies pie mācītā noteikuma.** Pēc *said / told / asked* pagātnē laiks iet soli atpakaļ, un tikai šo formu ieskaita. *He asked me if I like jazz* netiek pieņemts (b1-cond-rep-1:207 un līdzīgie). Skaidrojums pasaka, kāpēc: *After a past reporting verb (asked, said) the tense goes one step back: like → liked. This is always correct. In everyday English the present is also heard while something is still true; here we practise the rule.* LV: *Pēc ievadošā darbības vārda pagātnē (asked, said) laiks iet soli atpakaļ: like → liked. Tā vienmēr ir pareizi. Sarunvalodā dzird arī tagadni, ja teiktais joprojām ir spēkā; šeit trenējam noteikumu.* Murphy (48. nodaļa) to apstiprina: pagātnes forma der vienmēr, tagadne tikai tad, ja situācija nav mainījusies.
- **Noteikumu kartes piemērs ar piebildi** (*"I'm working late," he said.*) paliek kā izņēmums (Inga, 11. novembris).
- **Sākums *Tom told me* (b1-cond-rep-1:172):** sākums tagad dod *told me*, bet skaidrojums māca atšķirību *say / tell*. Ja gribi, lai izglītojamais izvēlas pats, norāde var būt *Tom's words to me: '…' Report them.* un pieņemtas abas: *Tom told me…* un *Tom said he would call me…*.

## F. Papildu atradumi esošajās atslēgās (nav saistīti ar piebildi)
