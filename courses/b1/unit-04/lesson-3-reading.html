📖 Unit 4 · Reading (exam practice)

💡 Exam tips

  1. Read the title and look at the text quickly.
  2. Read each question. Then find the part of the text that answers it.
  3. Check all three options. Wrong options often use words from the text, but they say something different.
  4. Some questions are True / False / Not given. Not given means the text does not say it.

📄 Should you go to university?

Every year, thousands of Latvian school leavers have to decide: should they go to university or start work? Both choices have advantages, and there is no right answer for everybody.

Students with a degree usually earn more money. According to recent statistics, graduates earn about 30% more than people with only secondary education. A degree also gives you qualifications for many professions, such as doctors or lawyers, where you must have a certificate. In addition, university is a great place to meet new people and learn independently.

On the other hand, university is expensive and takes a lot of time. Many students have to work part-time to pay their rent. Some students finish university with no work experience, and it can be difficult to find a job. "I should have started working earlier," says Elina, 25, who studied history for five years. "All the job advertisements asked for experience."

Today there are more alternatives. You don't have to study at university to learn a skill. You can do a vocational course, an apprenticeship or even free online courses. Daniels, 22, did a one-year IT course and now works full-time as a web developer. "I earn a good salary and I don't have any student debt," he says.

The advice of career experts is: think about what you enjoy, talk to people who work in jobs you like, and don't decide in a hurry.
