New: quantity words for shopping and money

  • Countable (can count: apples, euros, shops): many, a few, fewer. How many shops are there?
  • Uncountable (cannot count: money, time, milk): much, a little, less. How much money do you have?
  • Both: a lot of, some, any, enough, plenty of. I don't have enough money.
  • too + adjective (more than we want): too expensive. enough: cheap enough (adjective + enough), enough money (enough + noun).

Passive voice (a small introduction)

We use the passive when the action is more important than the person who does it: The shop is opened at 9. The bags were made in Italy. Form: be + past participle.
