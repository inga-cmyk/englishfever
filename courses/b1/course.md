# English Fever B1 – Intermediate English

| Unit | Topic (vocabulary) | Grammar | Writing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Daily life and free time | Present simple/continuous; past simple/present perfect | Emails about routines and clubs |
| 2 | Travel and holidays | Past continuous, past perfect, linkers | Email about a holiday; complaint |
| 3 | Technology and the future | will, going to, present continuous for future | Email about plans; enquiry |
| 4 | Work and education | must, have to, should, might, can't | Email about a job; application |
| 5 | People and places | comparatives, superlatives, (not) as ... as | Email describing a city; booking |
| 6 | Health and food | zero, first, second conditionals | Email with advice; request |
| 7 | News, media and environment | reported speech, question tags | Email passing on news; opinion |
| 8 | Money, shopping and review | mixed review (all tenses and structures) | Email about a purchase; refund |
