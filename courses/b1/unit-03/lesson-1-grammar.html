🧩 Unit 3 · Grammar: Talking about the future: will, going to, present continuous

🎯 Goal

You will learn to talk about plans, arrangements, predictions and quick decisions.

📘 1. Three ways to talk about the future

FormUse it forExample
will + verbquick decisions, offers, promises, predictions (opinions)It's cold. I'll close the window.
I think robots will do many jobs.
be going to + verbplans and intentions; predictions with evidenceI'm going to buy a new phone.
Look at those clouds! It's going to rain.
present continuous (am/is/are + -ing)fixed arrangements (time and place are decided)I'm meeting Anna at 6 p.m. tomorrow.

📘 2. How to choose

  • Did you decide before speaking? Use going to. I'm going to learn Spanish.
  • Do you decide now, while speaking? Use will. Oh no, I forgot milk! I'll go to the shop.
  • Is it in your calendar, with a time or place? Use the present continuous. We're flying to Oslo on Friday.

📘 3. Negatives and questions

will: I won't (will not) forget. Will you help me?
going to: She isn't going to come. Are you going to study tonight?

📘 4. Useful future words

Words for predictions: probably, definitely, maybe, perhaps, I think, I expect, I'm sure. Put probably after will: It will probably rain. But before won't: It probably won't rain.

⏰ Time words

tomorrow, next week, in two years, soon, by 2030, in the future.

⚠️ Common mistake

After when, as soon as, before, if we do not use will. Say When I arrive, I'll call you (not When I will arrive).

🗣️ Speaking practice

  1. What are you doing this weekend? Give details.
  2. What are you going to do next summer? Why?
  3. What do you think the world will be like in twenty years?
  4. What will you probably do after this class?
  5. You are in a café. What will you order? Say it with I'll.
  6. What are your plans for your next birthday?
  7. Your friend is going to start a new job. What do you think will happen?
  8. Do you think robots will take our jobs? Why or why not?
  9. Tell me about something you are going to learn or start soon. How are you going to do it?
  10. What is the weather going to be like tomorrow, and what will you do?
