## Variant 1 (recommended)
- **Task:** My grandparents ___ ___ in the same village for fifty years, and they still love it there.
- **Word bank (one word per gap):** have · are · lived · living
- **Answer:** have / lived
- **Real forms the bank also makes:** *are living* (now, but not up to now). *are lived* and *have living* do not fit.
- **Why (EN):** *For fifty years* and they still live there: the time goes up to now, so use *have* + 3rd form: *have lived*, not *are living*.
- **Why (LV):** *For fifty years*, un viņi joprojām tur dzīvo: laiks turpinās līdz šim brīdim, tāpēc lieto *have* + 3. formu: *have lived*, nevis *are living*.

## Variant 2 (your example)
- **Task:** We ___ ___ this car since 2018, and it still runs perfectly.
- **Word bank (one word per gap):** were · have · had · having
- **Answer:** have / had
- **Real forms the bank also makes:** *were having* (an action in progress in the past; *have* meaning "own" is not used like that).
- **Why (EN):** *Since 2018* and we still own it: up to now, use *have* + 3rd form: *have had*, not *were having*.
- **Why (LV):** *Since 2018*, un mašīna joprojām ir mūsu: līdz šim brīdim, tāpēc lieto *have* + 3. formu: *have had*, nevis *were having*.
