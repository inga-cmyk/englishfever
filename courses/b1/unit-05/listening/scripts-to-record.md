# Unit 5 listening scripts (record these; two speakers = two voices)

## Practice recording → b1-unit-05-listening-practice.mp3

Interviewer: Today we're asking people about their neighbourhood. Sofia, where do you live?
Sofia: I live in Āgenskalns, an area in Riga. It's quieter than the centre, but it's lively on Saturdays because there's a big market.
Interviewer: What's the best thing about it?
Sofia: Definitely the people. They're friendlier than in the centre, and everybody knows each other. The worst thing is the parking. It's very difficult to find a space.
Interviewer: And you, Mark?
Mark: I live in a village near Cēsis. It's much more peaceful than the city. I wake up to birdsong instead of traffic.
Interviewer: Is it expensive?
Mark: No, it's cheaper than Riga. My house cost half of what I would pay for a flat there. But I'm further from my work, so I spend two hours a day travelling.
Interviewer: Would you move?
Mark: Not now. I think the countryside is the best place for my children. The air is cleaner, and they have more space to play.

## Graded quiz recording → b1-unit-05-listening-quiz.mp3

Officer: Good afternoon. How can I help you?
Woman: Hello. I've lost my little brother in the shopping centre. He's eight years old.
Officer: Don't worry. We'll find him. What does he look like?
Woman: He's quite small for his age. He's got short, curly, fair hair and green eyes.
Officer: What is he wearing?
Woman: A red jacket, blue jeans and white trainers. And he's carrying a yellow backpack.
Officer: When did you last see him?
Woman: About half an hour ago, near the toy shop on the second floor. I turned around to look at a phone, and he wasn't there.
Officer: Is he shy, or would he talk to strangers?
Woman: He's more confident than most children. He'll probably ask somebody for help.
Officer: That's good. His name?
Woman: Jānis. Please hurry, I'm very worried.
Officer: We'll make an announcement right now. Please stay by the information desk, in case he comes back.
