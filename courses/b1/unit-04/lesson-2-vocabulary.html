🗣️ Speaking practice

  1. What job would you like to apply for? Why?
  2. Tell me about your best or worst job interview.
  3. Do you think salary is the most important thing at work? Why or why not?
  4. Describe a colleague you work well with.
  5. How do you deal with a tight deadline?
  6. What qualifications or experience do you have?
  7. Would you like to do a course or a degree? Which one, and why?
  8. What does your weekly timetable look like?
  9. Would you prefer full-time or part-time work? Why?
  10. What makes a good manager? What would make you resign or ask for a promotion?
