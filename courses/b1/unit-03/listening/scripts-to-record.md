# Unit 3 listening scripts (record these; two speakers = two voices)

## Practice recording → b1-unit-03-listening-practice.mp3

Dan: Hi Eva. Are you doing anything this weekend?
Eva: Hi Dan. Yes, I am. On Saturday morning I'm taking my laptop to the repair shop. The screen is broken.
Dan: Oh no! What are you going to do without a computer?
Eva: Well, my brother is going to lend me his tablet. In the afternoon I'm meeting my friend Kate.
Dan: Nice. What are you doing together?
Eva: We're going to try the new escape room in the old town. Do you want to come?
Dan: I'd love to, but I'm playing football on Saturday afternoon. What about Sunday?
Eva: On Sunday I'll probably stay at home and study for my exam. But I'll be free in the evening.
Dan: OK. I'll book a table at the new pizza place at seven o'clock. Shall I invite Kate too?
Eva: Yes, great idea! I'll send her a message now.

## Graded quiz recording → b1-unit-03-listening-quiz.mp3

Speaker: Good afternoon, everyone. Today I want to talk about what phones will be like in ten years' time.
First, I think phones will be much thinner and lighter. In fact, some companies are already making screens that you can fold like paper. Second, the battery will last much longer. Many experts believe that we will charge our phones only once a week.
Third, and most important, phones will become smarter. With artificial intelligence, your phone will know your habits. It will, for example, wake you up earlier if there is a traffic jam on your way to work. It will also translate your conversations in real time, so you will be able to speak to anyone in the world.
However, there are some risks. I'm worried that people will spend even more time on their screens, and young people may find it harder to concentrate. That is why I'm going to start a project in my school: next month, we are going to have one phone-free day every week.
Thank you for listening. Are there any questions?
