# gp5_will_going: Will or going to (A2.2), 2 new tasks

Topic: *Future: will, going to, present continuous*, level A2.2. Murphy unit 23 (A: *I'll* for a decision at the moment of speaking, *Oh, I forgot. I'll phone her now*; B: *going to* for something decided before, *Why are you filling that bucket? I'm going to wash the car*).

## Mix and why
2 typed gaps (use), no other kind. The point had 6 check tasks (2 choices, 3 gaps, 1 word order) and 2 use tasks, both at B1.2 (one names *be going to* in the instruction, one is an open writing task). It had no typed task at A2.2, and nearly all of its tasks are the *going to* prediction from evidence (clouds, ladder, sky). So the two new tasks are typed, one for each side of the contrast (a decision now, a plan made before), and neither is about evidence.
I did not write a whole-sentence task. I tried one (*You decided last week to sell your car. A friend asks …*), but every version also allows *I want to sell it*, *I've decided to sell it* and the present continuous. The task would then not test will against going to.
The instruction names both forms (*Complete with *will* or *going to**), like *Write *won't* or *wouldn't** and *Write *a few* or *a little** in batches 4 and 5. Choosing between them is the task, so naming both gives nothing away. It also keeps *must write*, *can write*, *want to buy* and *I'm buying* (all correct English) out of the open gap.

## Tasks
1. *'Did you reply to Mark's email?' 'Oh no, I forgot! I ___ to him now.' (write)*: key *'ll write | will write*. The learner must know that a decision made while speaking takes *will*. *Oh no, I forgot!* shows the decision is new. *going to write* competes because it is the plan form learners overuse (and Latvian has one future form for both).
2. *'Why are you saving so much money?' 'I ___ a flat next year.' (buy)*: key *'m going to buy | am going to buy*. The learner must know that an intention decided earlier takes *going to*. The saving shows the decision came before. *will buy* competes because *next year* + future makes many learners write *will*. Setting: money and lifestyle (task 1 is work and relationships).

## Card lines needed
None. The card already has *Decided before: going to* (*I'm going to visit my aunt this weekend. (I decided last week.)*) and *Decided now: will* (*I'm thirsty. I'll have some water.*).

## Doubts
- Task 1: a blind reader may call *I'm going to write to him now* "possible, less natural". Murphy 23A gives exactly this situation (*I forgot … I'll phone her now*) with *will* only, so I left it out of the key.
- Task 2: *I'm going to buy* without *am / 'm* (*I going to buy*) is refused on purpose; it is the typical form mistake on the card.
