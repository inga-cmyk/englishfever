Unit 4 · Listening (exam practice)

Exam tips: 1) Read the questions before you listen. 2) You hear the recording twice. 3) Listen for the answer, not for every word. 4) Write an answer for every question.

A job interview

Audio: [audio src="/wp-content/uploads/english-fever/b1-unit-04-listening-practice.mp3"]

Listen twice. Then answer the questions.

Activity:

[h5p id="u04-listening-practice"]
Show the script (read it after you finish)

Interviewer: Good morning, Anna. Please sit down. Thank you for coming.
Anna: Good morning. Thank you for inviting me.
Interviewer: First, can you tell me about your experience?
Anna: Yes. For two years I worked as a sales assistant in a clothes shop. I had to help customers and organise the stock.
Interviewer: Why did you leave that job?
Anna: I wanted more responsibility. I think I should learn new skills, and your company has a good training programme.
Interviewer: I see. Our job is full-time. You would have to work on Saturdays about twice a month. Is that a problem?
Anna: No, that's fine. I don't have to look after anyone at home, so I'm free.
Interviewer: Good. The salary is nine hundred euros a month before tax. When could you start?
Anna: I must give one month's notice to my current boss, so I can start on the first of March.
Interviewer: That's perfect. We will call you by Friday.
