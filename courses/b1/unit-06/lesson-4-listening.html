🎧 Unit 6 · Listening (exam practice)

💡 Exam tips: multiple choice

  1. Read the questions and the three options before you listen.
  2. Listen for the main idea, not for every word.
  3. Wrong options may use a word from the recording, but they say something different.
  4. Choose one answer for every question, even if you are not sure.

📜 Script

Show the script (read it after you finish)

Doctor: Good morning, Mr Ozols. What seems to be the problem?
Patient: Good morning, doctor. I have a terrible headache and a sore throat. I feel very tired too.
Doctor: How long have you felt like this?
Patient: For about three days.
Doctor: Let me take your temperature. Hmm, it's thirty-eight point five. You have a fever. Do you have a cough?
Patient: Yes, a little. Mostly at night.
Doctor: I think you have the flu. If you rest for a few days and drink a lot of water, you will recover in a week.
Patient: Do I need any medicine?
Doctor: I'll give you a prescription for some tablets. Take one in the morning and one in the evening, after eating. If your temperature gets higher, call me.
Patient: Can I go to work tomorrow?
Doctor: I wouldn't, if I were you. Stay at home for at least three days, or you might make other people ill. I'll write a note for your employer.
Patient: Thank you, doctor.
