# Blind check r11 and r11b: the judge's decisions (batch 4, B1.2 part, 15 November)

150 tasks (`pending/gap-points-b12-1.csv`), two readers; 71 flagged. Nothing needed the teacher.

- **Complaint tasks with *always / constantly* (r11-008, 009, 013, 014):** both readers: the simple form is a correct complaint too. Accepted (*always leave*, *always hummed*, *constantly calls*, *always borrowed*), each with its own answer note: the simple form says it happens often; *always* + *-ing* adds the annoyance. Same rule as *must / have to* (15 November): a form that is correct English is never marked wrong.
- **Two-gap tasks (r11-010 printer, r11-011 car):** typed, they also took *has jammed / will jam* and *would break / had broken*; the grader checks each gap alone, so pairs cannot be keyed. Rebuilt as word banks (*is · jamming · was · jams*; *was · breaking · is · breaks*); r11b: both readers solved them; *was always jamming* beside *We need a new printer* stays wrong.
- **r11-017 order:** the cards also made *i always want to go cycling when it's raining*. Rebuilt: *my neighbour is always playing loud music late at night*; r11b: the yes/no question with the same cards is accepted too; an order split by the alternative-order tool (*at night … late*) removed.
- **r11-062 question order:** the people could swap (*what should my boss have said when I asked…*), 12 cards. Rebuilt: *what should i have said to my boss* (r11b, no problem).
- **Accepted answers:** *during meetings*, *Every Saturday my father would cook…*, *loaned him your car*, *My flight was cancelled, so I spent the whole day…*, *lasts for ninety minutes*, *a boat ticket for tomorrow's trip*, *all day we were stuck…*, *I missed the whole match because I spent the evening…*, *All that she told me…*, *This file won't open on my computer*.
- **Kept:** *always is borrowing* and *always was complaining* (not standard order), *would live / would know* with states (the point), *didn't used to like* (a common mistake, wrong option by the rule of 11 November), *could have told* for *(should / tell)*, *shouldn't have drank*, *which* where the instruction says *what* or *that*, *wouldn't* for *Can you help me? The zip ___ close*, "—" as the option for no word (house style), long orders of 13-14 cards (within 10-16 words for B1.2), orders that change the meaning.
