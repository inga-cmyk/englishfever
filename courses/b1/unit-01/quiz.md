# Unit 1 Quiz (Question Set source)

Correct answer marked with `*`.

1. She usually ______ coffee in the morning.
   - a) drinking  - b) *drinks  - c) is drinking
2. Be quiet! The baby ______.
   - a) sleeps  - b) *is sleeping  - c) has slept
3. I ______ in Riga since 2015.
   - a) lived  - b) am living  - c) *have lived
4. We ______ to Paris last summer.
   - a) *went  - b) have been  - c) have gone
5. ______ you ever ______ sushi?
   - a) Did / eat  - b) *Have / eaten  - c) Are / eating
6. He ______ English very well. (state verb)
   - a) is understanding  - b) *understands  - c) understood
7. I ______ my homework yet.
   - a) didn't finish  - b) am not finishing  - c) *haven't finished
8. Which sentence is correct?
   - a) I have seen him yesterday.  - b) *I saw him yesterday.  - c) I am seeing him yesterday.
9. They ______ football every Saturday.
   - a) *play  - b) are playing  - c) have played
10. "I took up yoga" means:
   - a) I stopped yoga  - b) *I started yoga  - c) I watched yoga
