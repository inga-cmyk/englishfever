# Unit 5 interview cards – People and places

**Card 1.** Can you describe your best friend?

_Ideas:_ My best friend is ... He/She is quite tall / has curly hair ... He/She is the most ... person I know.

**Card 2.** Who is the most important person in your life? Why?

_Ideas:_ Say who, how long you've known them, and why they matter. Use the superlative: the kindest / the most helpful.

**Card 3.** What is your hometown like?

_Ideas:_ It's a ... town in ... It's smaller / quieter than Riga. The best thing about it is ...

**Card 4.** Do you prefer living in a city or in the countryside?

_Ideas:_ I prefer ... because it's quieter / more convenient. On the other hand ...

**Card 5.** How is your neighbourhood different from the city centre?

_Ideas:_ It's less crowded / cleaner / more peaceful than ... There are fewer ... but more ...

**Card 6.** Which place in your country would you recommend to tourists?

_Ideas:_ I would recommend ... It's the most beautiful place in ... You can ... It's famous for ...

**Card 7.** Who in your family do you look like?

_Ideas:_ I look like my ... We both have ... but I'm taller / shorter than ...

**Card 8.** What is the biggest difference between you and your friends?

_Ideas:_ I'm more ... than ... but I'm not as ... as ...

**Card 9.** Describe the place where you grew up.

_Ideas:_ When I was a child, we lived in ... It was ... I remember ...

**Card 10.** What makes a good neighbour?

_Ideas:_ A good neighbour is friendly, quiet and helpful. They shouldn't ...

**Card 11.** What are the best and worst things about living in your city?

_Ideas:_ The best thing is ... but the worst thing is ... Use superlatives.

**Card 12.** If you could live anywhere, where would it be?

_Ideas:_ I'd live in ... because it's warmer / cheaper / more exciting. (Unit 6: second conditional)
