# Blind check r16b: flags for the judge

1 tasks, 2 readers.

## r16b-001 · A2.2 · gap · built-in

Task: Hurry up! If you ___ the bus, you ___ late. (miss / be)
Key: {"kind": "gap", "accepted": [["miss"], ["will be", "'ll be", "are going to be", "'re going to be", "might be", "may be", "could be"]]}
- reader 1: another correct answer, not accepted: [['missed', 'would be'], ['missed', "'d be"]]
- reader 1: instruction: Does not say which tense or whether the situation is real, so the second conditional (missed / would be) and modals (might/may/could be) are also correct English; 'Hurry up!' makes first conditional most natural but does not exclude them. The key should accept contractions ('ll be) and 'are going to be'.
- reader 2: another correct answer, not accepted: [['miss', 'are'], ['missed', 'would be'], ['missed', "'d be"]]
- reader 2: instruction: Verb pairs only, so the learner must decide the form; fine. But the second gap needs two words (will be) while the first needs one, and nothing tells the learner that; typed 'll be vs will be and are going to be all work, so the key must accept them. Present 'are' (zero conditional) is correct but less natural after 'Hurry up!'; second-conditional 'missed / would be' is correct English but does not fit the urgent real situation.
- reader 2: level: First conditional with will is borderline for A2.2 (usually B1 start); sentence length and words are fine.
