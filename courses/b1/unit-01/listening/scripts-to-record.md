# Unit 1 listening scripts (record these; two speakers = two voices)

## Practice recording → b1-unit-01-listening-practice.mp3

Tom: Hi Lena! Long time no see. What have you been doing?
Lena: Hi Tom! I'm busy. I've just started a new job, so I commute for an hour every day.
Tom: Wow. Do you like it?
Lena: Yes, I do. But I don't have much time for hobbies now. What about you?
Tom: Well, I took up cycling last month. I go out every Saturday morning with a group of friends.
Lena: That sounds great! How far do you cycle?
Tom: About thirty kilometres. Then we have lunch together. Actually, we're meeting this Saturday at the lake. Why don't you come?
Lena: I'd love to, but I can't. I'm visiting my grandmother in Liepāja.
Tom: What about Sunday, then? We could go to the cinema.
Lena: Sunday is perfect! There's a new comedy at the Splendid Palace. Let's go at six o'clock.
Tom: Great. I'll buy the tickets online. See you on Sunday!

## Graded quiz recording → b1-unit-01-listening-quiz.mp3

Presenter: Today we're talking to Ieva, who runs a club for people who want to try something new. Welcome, Ieva.
Ieva: Thank you.
Presenter: Why did you start the club?
Ieva: Three years ago I moved to Riga and I didn't know anyone. I wanted to meet people, so I invited my neighbours to play board games. Eight people came, and now we have over sixty members.
Presenter: What do you do in the club?
Ieva: Every month we try a different activity. This month it's pottery. Last month we went climbing, and I was really afraid at first, but I loved it!
Presenter: Is it expensive?
Ieva: No. It costs five euros a month. The money pays for the room and the materials.
Presenter: And who joins your club?
Ieva: All kinds of people, from students to retired teachers. The most important thing is to be curious. Many people say that they have never had time for hobbies, but they find that a hobby helps them relax after work.
Presenter: How can people join?
Ieva: Just visit our website. The first evening is free, so you can see if you like it. We meet every second Thursday at seven.
