Unit 5 · Listening (exam practice)

Exam tips: 1) Read the questions before you listen. 2) You hear the recording twice. 3) Listen for the answer, not for every word. 4) Write an answer for every question.

Two people talk about where they live

Audio: [audio src="/wp-content/uploads/english-fever/b1-unit-05-listening-practice.mp3"]

Listen twice. Then answer the questions.

Activity:

[h5p id="u05-listening-practice"]
Show the script (read it after you finish)

Interviewer: Today we're asking people about their neighbourhood. Sofia, where do you live?
Sofia: I live in Āgenskalns, an area in Riga. It's quieter than the centre, but it's lively on Saturdays because there's a big market.
Interviewer: What's the best thing about it?
Sofia: Definitely the people. They're friendlier than in the centre, and everybody knows each other. The worst thing is the parking. It's very difficult to find a space.
Interviewer: And you, Mark?
Mark: I live in a village near Cēsis. It's much more peaceful than the city. I wake up to birdsong instead of traffic.
Interviewer: Is it expensive?
Mark: No, it's cheaper than Riga. My house cost half of what I would pay for a flat there. But I'm further from my work, so I spend two hours a day travelling.
Interviewer: Would you move?
Mark: Not now. I think the countryside is the best place for my children. The air is cleaner, and they have more space to play.
