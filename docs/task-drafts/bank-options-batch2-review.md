# Word-bank wrong options, batch 2: conditionals (draft for review)

Same rule as batch 1 (teacher, 8 November): the wrong chips build **real forms** that do not fit the sentence (*will heat* after *if*, *would* in a real condition, *had passed* instead of *would have passed*), no bare *-ing* and no forms that do not exist (*will arrives*).

Every new chip was checked: no other combination gives a correct sentence. Where a form like *will freeze* or *gets* would also be correct, it is **not** in the bank (column *Why*). No task text changes.

**59 tasks change, 14 stay.** Research data: drafts keep their id (only the options change, from the import date); the 2 built-in tasks get a new version.
