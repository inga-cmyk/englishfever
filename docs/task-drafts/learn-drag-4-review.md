## Present perfect or past simple · B1.1

6. *gap* · We ___ ___ this car since 2018, and it still runs perfectly. → **have / had** · words: have / had / were / having
   - EN: *Since 2018* and we still own it: up to now, use *have* + 3rd form: *have had*, not *were having*.
   - LV: *Since 2018*, un mašīna joprojām ir mūsu: līdz šim brīdim, tāpēc lieto *have* + 3. formu: *have had*, nevis *were having*.
   - sentence from: compare-past-1.csv · line 28
7. *gap* · I ___ there from 2010 to 2014. Now I work in Riga. → **worked** · words: worked / have worked / work
   - EN: A finished time (*from 2010 to 2014*, *yesterday*): past simple.
   - LV: Pabeigts laiks (*from 2010* to 2014, *yesterday*): Past Simple.
   - sentence from: compare-past-1.csv · line 25
8. *gap* · I ___ here since 2010. I still work here. → **have worked** · words: have worked / worked / work
   - EN: Up to now (*since 2010*, *this morning* while it is still morning): *have* + 3rd form.
   - LV: Līdz šim brīdim (*since 2010, this morning*, kamēr vēl ir rīts): *have* + 3. forma.
   - sentence from: compare-past-1.csv · line 24
9. *order* · Put the words in order. → **I have never ridden a horse**
   - EN: Your whole life up to now: *have never* + 3rd form.
   - LV: Visa dzīve līdz šim: *have never* + 3. forma.
   - sentence from: compare-past-1.csv · line 19
