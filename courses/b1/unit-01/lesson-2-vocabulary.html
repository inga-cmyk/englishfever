🗣️ Speaking practice

  1. How do you commute to work or school? How long does it take?
  2. Where do you like to hang out with friends, and when do you get together?
  3. Who would you like to catch up with? What would you tell them?
  4. What did you get into recently? Why do you like it?
  5. Would you like to take up a new hobby? Which one, and why?
  6. Describe your daily routine in four or five sentences.
  7. What do you do to relax after a busy day?
  8. When do you stay up late, and how do you feel the next day?
  9. Are you a member of a club or group? What do you do there?
  10. What do you do in your spare time to keep fit? How often do you use social media?
