🎯 Goal

You will learn 16 useful words and phrases about People and places.

🔤 Word list

Word / phraseTypeMeaningExample
friendlyadjectivekind and pleasant to othersThe people here are very friendly.
shyadjectivenervous with other peopleHe is shy and doesn't talk much.
generousadjectivehappy to give money or timeShe is generous with her time.
reliableadjectivesomeone you can trustTom is a reliable friend.
talladjectivehigh (for a person)My father is very tall.
slimadjectivethin in an attractive wayShe is tall and slim.
curlyadjectivenot straight (hair)He has curly brown hair.
crowdedadjectivefull of peopleThe beach was crowded.
peacefuladjectivequiet and calmThe village is peaceful.
historicadjectiveimportant in historyRiga has a historic centre.
suburbnounan area outside the centre of a cityWe live in a quiet suburb.
neighbourhoodnounthe area around where you liveIt's a safe neighbourhood.
skyscrapernouna very tall buildingNew York is famous for its skyscrapers.
cosyadjectivesmall, warm and comfortableThe café is small and cosy.
livelyadjectivefull of energy and lifeIt's a lively city with great nightlife.
pollutedadjectivedirty because of chemicals or smokeThe air is polluted in big cities.

🗣️ Speaking practice

  1. Describe a friendly person and a shy person you know.
  2. Who is the most generous or reliable person you know? What do they do?
  3. Describe a person in your family: height, hair and character.
  4. What is your neighbourhood like? Is it peaceful or lively?
  5. Do you live in the city centre or in a suburb? What do you like about it?
  6. Describe a historic place near you.
  7. Which places in your town are crowded, and when?
  8. Is the air polluted in your town? What could change that?
  9. Would you like to live near skyscrapers? Why or why not?
  10. Describe a cosy place where you like to spend time.
