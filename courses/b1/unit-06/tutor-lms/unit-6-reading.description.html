Letter to a health magazine

Dear Dr Brown,

I am 35 years old, and I work as an accountant. I spend most of my day sitting at my desk, and in the evening I am usually too tired to do anything except watch TV. Recently I have been having headaches nearly every day. I also find it difficult to fall asleep, and I'm often stressed. If I don't change something, I will be in real trouble. What should I do?

Signed, Tired of being tired

Dr Brown replies:

Dear Tired,

First, I should say that if the headaches continue, you should see your doctor, because there could be a medical reason. In the meantime, there are a few simple things which can help.

Many people with desk jobs get headaches because they don't move enough or because they look at a screen for hours. If I were you, I would take a five-minute break every hour. Stand up, stretch and look out of the window. If you can, walk for 20 minutes at lunchtime: it will help you feel less stressed.

Also, if you drink coffee after 4 p.m., it might be the reason you can't sleep. Try switching to herbal tea in the evening. And unless you turn off your phone an hour before bed, your brain won't relax. Finally, you don't have to join an expensive gym. Even a short walk in the evening will make a difference.

Good luck – and write again to tell me how you are!
