🔤 Word list

Word / phraseTypeMeaningExample
devicenouna machine or small electronic objectMy phone is my favourite device.
downloadverbcopy a file from the internet to your deviceI downloaded a new app.
uploadverbsend a file from your device to the internetShe uploaded the photos to the cloud.
softwarenounprograms that run on a computerYou need to update the software.
passwordnouna secret word to open an accountChoose a strong password.
screennounthe flat part of a phone or computer where you see thingsThe screen is cracked.
chargeverbput electricity into a batteryI need to charge my phone.
artificial intelligencenouncomputer systems that can learn and decideArtificial intelligence can translate languages.
onlineadjectiveconnected to the internetI do my shopping online.
websitenouna place on the internet with pages of informationVisit our website for details.
appnouna program on a phoneThere's an app for everything.
connectionnouna link to the internetMy internet connection is slow.
deleteverbremove or eraseI deleted the old emails.
inventverbmake something new for the first timeWho invented the telephone?
updateverbmake something newerPlease update your app.
searchverblook for information on the internetI searched for a recipe.

🗣️ Speaking practice

  1. Which device do you use most every day? What do you use it for?
  2. Which app did you download recently? Why?
  3. How do you keep your passwords safe?
  4. How many hours a day do you look at a screen? How do you feel about that?
  5. When do you charge your phone? What do you do when the battery is low?
  6. How do you use artificial intelligence in your life or work?
  7. What do you usually search for online?
  8. Have you had problems with a bad connection? What did you do?
  9. What do you usually delete or update on your phone or computer?
  10. Which invention has changed our lives the most? Why?
