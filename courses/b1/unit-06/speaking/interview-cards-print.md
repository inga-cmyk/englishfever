# Unit 6 interview cards – Health and food

**Card 1.** What do you usually eat for breakfast, lunch and dinner?

_Ideas:_ For breakfast I usually have ... At lunchtime I ... In the evening we ... Use frequency words: usually, sometimes.

**Card 2.** Do you think you have a healthy diet? Why or why not?

_Ideas:_ I think my diet is quite healthy / could be better because I eat ... but I also ...

**Card 3.** What is your favourite dish? Can you tell me how to make it?

_Ideas:_ First you need ... Then you ... Finally ... Use the imperative and sequence words.

**Card 4.** What would you do if you felt ill the day before an important exam?

_Ideas:_ If I felt ill, I would ... (second conditional)

**Card 5.** How do you stay healthy?

_Ideas:_ I try to ... I go to ... I make sure that I ... If I don't sleep well, I ...

**Card 6.** What traditional Latvian food would you recommend to a visitor?

_Ideas:_ I'd recommend ... It's made of ... It tastes ... You can try it in ...

**Card 7.** Have you ever been to a doctor or hospital? What happened?

_Ideas:_ Present perfect or past simple: I've been to ... once. I had to ... because I had ...

**Card 8.** Do you prefer eating at home or in restaurants? Why?

_Ideas:_ I prefer ... because it's cheaper / healthier. On the other hand ...

**Card 9.** What advice would you give to someone who wants to lose weight?

_Ideas:_ If I were you, I would ... You should ... You shouldn't ... It's important to ...

**Card 10.** If you could eat one food for the rest of your life, what would it be?

_Ideas:_ I would choose ... because ... I would never get bored of it.

**Card 11.** Do you think fast food should be banned near schools? Why?

_Ideas:_ In my opinion ... If children eat too much fast food, they will ...

**Card 12.** What do you do when you feel stressed?

_Ideas:_ When I'm stressed, I ... It helps me to relax because ...
