In sport, the Latvian basketball team won last night's match against Estonia, seventy-eight to seventy-one. The coach said that he was very proud of his players.

And finally, the weather. The forecast says it will be cloudy with some rain in the morning, but the afternoon will be sunny and warm, with temperatures up to twenty-two degrees. ## Graded quiz recording → b1-unit-07-listening-quiz.mp3

Ella: Hi Markus. You're sorting the rubbish, aren't you?
Markus: Yes, I am. I've just started recycling. My neighbour told me that it was really easy, and she gave me some bags.
Ella: Great idea! How does it work?
Markus: There are three bins: one for paper, one for plastic, and one for glass. The city collects them every Wednesday.
Ella: And what about food waste?
Markus: I asked the council about that, and they said that it would be possible to get a special bin from next month. In the meantime, I'm making compost in the garden.
Ella: Good for you. I wish I could do more, but I live in a flat.
Markus: You can still recycle. Take your old batteries to the supermarket, for example. They have a special box next to the exit.
Ella: I didn't know that! And I always buy water in plastic bottles, don't I? Maybe I should use a reusable one.
Markus: Definitely. If everyone did that, we would reduce plastic waste a lot.
