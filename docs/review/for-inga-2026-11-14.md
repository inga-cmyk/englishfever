# Jūsu lēmumam: 13 pārbūves un 21 lomas maiņa (14. novembris)

## 1. Pārbūvējamie uzdevumi (13)

Lielākajā daļā viena nepareizā atbilde patiesībā ir pareiza angļu valoda. Ieteikums ir pie katra uzdevuma.

1. **In an email to a client you want to sound confident but polite. Choose the correct option.** (B1.2)  
   Pareizā: *If you send us the documents today, we will process your request by Friday.* Nepareizās: *If you will send us the documents today, we will process your request by Friday. / If you sent us the documents today, we will process your request by Friday. / If you send us the documents today, we would process your request by Friday.*  
   Kāpēc: *If you will send…* (pieklājīga gatavība) un *If you sent… (šorīt)* arī ir pareiza angļu valoda. Ieteikums: situāciju noņemt; šīs divas aizstāt ar *If you will sending us…* un *If you would send us…, we will process…*.

2. **Choose the correct option: I didn't charge my phone last night. Imagine a different past and its result now.** (B2.1)  
   Pareizā: *If I had charged my phone, it would be working now.* Nepareizās: *If I had charge my phone, it would be working now. / If I had charged my phone, it would working now. / If I had charged my phone, it would be work now.*  
   Kāpēc: nepareizās atbildes nav jaukta nosacījuma (pagātne / tagadne) kļūdas. Ieteikums: situāciju atstāt; nepareizās: 'If I charged my phone, it would be working now.' / 'If I had charged my phone, it would have been working now.' / 'If I would have charged my phone, it would be working now.'

3. **A colleague forgot to save a file and lost two hours of work. Choose the correct comment about what could have been different.** (B1.2)  
   Pareizā: *If you had saved it every ten minutes, you wouldn't have lost so much work.* Nepareizās: *If you saved it every ten minutes, you wouldn't have lost so much work. / If you had saved it every ten minutes, you won't lose so much work. / If you would have saved it every ten minutes, you wouldn't have lost so much work.*  
   Kāpēc: *If you saved it every ten minutes, you wouldn't have lost…* ir pareizs jaukts nosacījums. Ieteikums: aizstāt ar *If you had save it every ten minutes…*, tad situāciju noņemt: *Which sentence is correct?*

4. **You want to know if paying is necessary. Which question is correct?** (B1.1)  
   Pareizā: *Do I have to pay now?* Nepareizās: *Have I to pay now? / Do I must pay now? / Must I to pay now?*  
   Kāpēc: *Have I to pay now?* pastāv vecākā britu angļu valodā. Ieteikums: aizstāt ar *Do I has to pay now?*, tad: *Which question is correct?*

5. **You want to say you planned something but did not do it. Choose the best option.** (B1.2)  
   Pareizā: *I was going to buy a car, but I changed my mind.* Nepareizās: *I am going to buy a car, but I changed my mind. / I went to buy a car, but I changed my mind. / I will buy a car, but I changed my mind.*  
   Kāpēc: *I went to buy a car, but I changed my mind* ir pareizs un der. Ieteikums: aizstāt ar *I was go to buy a car, but I changed my mind.*, tad: *Which sentence is correct?*

6. **You cannot open the door because your keys are missing. Which sentence most directly presents the loss as a current problem?** (B1.1)  
   Pareizā: *I've lost my keys.* Nepareizās: *I lost my keys. / Both tell us the same. / Neither tells us this.*  
   Kāpēc: divas atbildes ir komentāri, nevis teikumi, un *I lost my keys* arī ir pareizi. Ieteikums: I can't open the door. I ___ my keys. (have lost / lose / am losing / had lost)

7. **Your boss asks why you look tired. You were in traffic for an hour until now. Choose the correct option.** (B1.2)  
   Pareizā: *I've been sitting in traffic for an hour.* Nepareizās: *I sit in traffic for an hour. / I will sit in traffic for an hour. / I sat in traffic tomorrow.*  
   Kāpēc: nepareizās atbildes nav ticamas kļūdas (*I sat in traffic tomorrow*). Ieteikums: Sorry I'm late. I ___ in traffic since eight. (have been sitting / sit / am sitting / sat)

8. **You want to write a neutral email and not blame anyone. Choose the correct option.** (B1.1)  
   Pareizā: *The invoices are sent on Fridays.* Nepareizās: *You send the invoices on Fridays. / Someone sends the invoices on Fridays. / I think you send the invoices on Fridays.*  
   Kāpēc: *Someone sends the invoices on Fridays* arī nevienu nevaino. Ieteikums: aizstāt ar *The invoices are send on Fridays.*, situāciju atstāt.

9. **Which sentence correctly reports 'I'm sorry I'm late'?** (B2.2)  
   Pareizā: *He apologised for being late.* Nepareizās: *He apologised that he was late. / He apologised to be late. / He apologised of being late.*  
   Kāpēc: *He apologised that he was late* lieto britu angļu valodā. Ieteikums: aizstāt ar *He apologised for be late.*, tad: *Which sentence is correct?*

10. **Which sentence says what happened to the shop, without saying who did it?** (A2.2)  
   Pareizā: *The shop was robbed last night.* Nepareizās: *Someone robbed the shop last night. / The robbers took money from the shop. / We went to the shop last night.*  
   Kāpēc: *Someone …* arī nepasaka, kas to izdarīja. Ieteikums: aizstāt ar *The shop robbed last night.*; paliek pārbaudes uzdevums.

11. **Which sentence says that someone stole my phone, without saying who?** (B1.2)  
   Pareizā: *My phone was stolen on the bus.* Nepareizās: *Someone stole my phone on the bus. / The thief stole my phone. / I lost my phone on the bus.*  
   Kāpēc: *Someone …* arī nepasaka, kas to izdarīja. Ieteikums: aizstāt ar *My phone stole on the bus.*; paliek pārbaudes uzdevums.

12. **They ___ to move to the coast, but they haven't decided yet. (think)** (B1.1, rakstāms)  
   Kāpēc: *thinking to move* nav dabiska angļu valoda. Ieteikums: They ___ about moving to the coast at the moment, but they haven't decided yet. (think) => are thinking

13. **These books ___ by children. (read)** (A2.2, rakstāms)  
   Kāpēc: *These books are read by children* ir dīvains paraugteikums. Ieteikums: These books ___ by millions of children every year. (read) => are read
