Doctor: Good morning, Mr Ozols. What seems to be the problem?
Patient: Good morning, doctor. I have a terrible headache and a sore throat. I feel very tired too.
Doctor: How long have you felt like this?
Patient: For about three days.
Doctor: Let me take your temperature. Hmm, it's thirty-eight point five. You have a fever. Do you have a cough?
Patient: Yes, a little. Mostly at night.
Doctor: I think you have the flu. If you rest for a few days and drink a lot of water, you will recover in a week.
Patient: Do I need any medicine?
Doctor: I'll give you a prescription for some tablets. Take one in the morning and one in the evening, after eating. If your temperature gets higher, call me.
Patient: Can I go to work tomorrow?
Doctor: I wouldn't, if I were you. Stay at home for at least three days, or you might make other people ill. I'll write a note for your employer.
Patient: Thank you, doctor.

## Graded quiz recording → b1-unit-06-listening-quiz.mp3

Presenter: Hello and welcome to Cooking with Dana. Today, I'm going to show you how to make a healthy vegetable soup. It's very easy, and you only need a few ingredients.
You need two carrots, one onion, three potatoes, a litre of water, and some salt and pepper. If you like, you can add garlic. Garlic is good for the heart, so I always add some.
First, wash and cut all the vegetables into small pieces. If the pieces are small, the soup will cook faster. Next, heat a little olive oil in a large pot and fry the onion for about three minutes. Then add the carrots and potatoes and the water.
Boil the soup for twenty minutes. If you want a smoother soup, you can use a blender. I like it chunky, so I don't blend it. Finally, add salt and pepper and serve it with fresh bread.
This soup is vegetarian, and it's also ideal for people who are allergic to nuts. If you make too much, don't worry. You can keep it in the fridge for three days. See you next week!
