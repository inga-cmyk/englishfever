Free time in Latvia: survey results

A survey asked 1,000 Latvians aged 18–35 about their free time. The results show some interesting changes.

The most popular free-time activity is spending time with family and friends (68%). Watching films or series is second (61%), and walking or being in nature is third (52%). Only 17% of people read books every week, which is lower than ten years ago.

Many young people say they have less free time than their parents had. On average they work 42 hours a week, and many also commute for more than an hour a day. "I get home at seven and I'm too tired to do anything," said one woman from Jelgava.

However, people are finding new ways to relax. One in four people has taken up a new hobby in the last year. The most popular new hobbies are cycling, cooking and board games. Others are learning languages online. Of those who started a new hobby, 80% say they feel happier.

The researchers believe that social media takes a lot of time: the average person spends two and a half hours a day on it. Almost half of the people in the survey would like to spend less time online.
