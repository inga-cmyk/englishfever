# Typed forms for B2 and C1: typical mistakes (41)

For the level test (type the form, nothing to guess) and for practice. Each task has one gap; the word in brackets is the base. *catches* = the typical wrong answer the task is there to find. Approved by the teacher on 6 November.


## B2.1

1. *If I ___ her number, I would call her right now. (know)* → **knew** · catches: *would know* · Second conditional (if + past, would)
   - EN: An imagined present: *if* + past (*knew*), not *would*.
   - LV: Iedomāta tagadne: *if* + pagātne (*knew*), nevis *would*. *Tulkojot burtiski* "ja es zinātu", *gribas would*, bet aiz *if* tā nav.
2. *If the train ___ faster, more people would use it for work. (be)* → **was | were** · catches: *would be* · Second conditional (if + past, would)
   - EN: An imagined present: *if* + past (*knew*), not *would*.
   - LV: Iedomāta tagadne: *if* + pagātne (*knew*), nevis *would*. *Tulkojot burtiski* "ja es zinātu", *gribas would*, bet aiz *if* tā nav.
3. *I ___ more if the gym were closer to my office. (exercise)* → **would exercise | 'd exercise | might exercise | could exercise** · catches: *will exercise* · Second conditional (if + past, would)
   - EN: The result of an imagined present: *would* (or *could, might*) + base form.
   - LV: Iedomātas tagadnes sekas: *would* (vai *could, might*) + pamatforma.
4. *If we ___ earlier, we wouldn't have missed the flight. (leave)* → **had left | 'd left** · catches: *would have left* · Third conditional (if + had done, would have done)
   - EN: An imagined past: *if* + *had* + 3rd form, not *would have*.
   - LV: Iedomāta pagātne: *if + had* + 3. forma, nevis *would have*.
5. *If you had asked me, I ___ you with the move. (help)* → **would have helped | 'd have helped | might have helped | could have helped** · catches: *would helped* · Third conditional (if + had done, would have done)
   - EN: The result in an imagined past: *would have* (or *could have, might have*) + 3rd form.
   - LV: Sekas iedomātā pagātnē: *would have* (vai *could have, might have*) + 3. forma.
6. *'I'll send the report tomorrow.' Tom said he ___ the report the next day. (send)* → **would send | 'd send** · catches: *will send* · Reported statements (tense changes)
   - EN: Reported later: *will* becomes *would*.
   - LV: Atstāstot vēlāk, *will* kļūst par *would*.
7. *'I've lived here for two years.' She told me she ___ there for two years. (live)* → **had lived | had been living | 'd lived | 'd been living** · catches: *has lived* · Reported statements (tense changes)
   - EN: Reported later: *have lived* becomes *had lived* (or *had been living*).
   - LV: Atstāstot vēlāk, *have lived* kļūst par *had lived* (vai *had been living*).
8. *The receptionist asked me where I ___ from. (be)* → **was** · catches: *am* · Reported questions and requests
   - EN: A reported question: statement order and one step back in time: *where I was from*.
   - LV: Atstāstītā jautājumā: *apgalvojuma* secība un laiks soli atpakaļ: *where I was from*.
9. *She asked me how long I ___ English. (study)* → **had been studying | had studied | 'd been studying | 'd studied** · catches: *have been studying* · Reported questions and requests
   - EN: A reported question: one step back in time: *had been studying*.
   - LV: Atstāstītā jautājumā laiks soli atpakaļ: *had been studying*.
10. *Could you tell me what time the museum ___ on Sundays? (open)* → **opens** · catches: *does open* · Indirect questions (Could you tell me where...)
   - EN: The embedded question has subject before verb and no *does*: *what time the museum opens*.
   - LV: Netiešajā jautājumā teikuma priekšmets ir pirms darbības vārda, un nav *does*: *what time the museum opens*.
11. *When I finally got through, I ___ on hold for forty minutes. (wait)* → **had been waiting | 'd been waiting | had waited | 'd waited** · catches: *was waiting* · Narrative tenses with past perfect continuous
   - EN: How long something went on before a moment in the past: *had been* + *-ing*.
   - LV: Cik ilgi kaut kas notika līdz kādam brīdim pagātnē: *had been + -ing*.
12. *___ her knee injury, she finished the marathon.* → **Despite** · catches: *Although* · Contrast, purpose and reason (although, despite, so that)
   - EN: *Despite* + noun (*her knee injury*); *although* and *even though* need subject + verb, and *despite* has no *of*.
   - LV: *Despite* + lietvārds (*her knee injury*); aiz *although* un *even though* vajag teikuma priekšmetu un darbības vārdu, un *despite* lieto bez *of*.
13. *I set two alarms in order ___ my early flight. (not / miss)* → **not to miss | to not miss** · catches: *to not forget* · Contrast, purpose and reason (although, despite, so that)
   - EN: Purpose, negative: *in order not to* + base form.
   - LV: Nolūks noliegumā: *in order not to* + pamatforma.
14. *I'll leave you my number in case you ___ any problems with the flat. (have)* → **have | should have** · catches: *will rain* · Contrast, purpose, reason and result (whereas, in case, therefore)
   - EN: After *in case* the present (or *should*), not *will*.
   - LV: Aiz *in case* tagadne (vai *should*), nevis *will*.

## B2.2

15. *I would have gone to the concert if I ___ about it. (know)* → **had known | 'd known** · catches: *would have known* · Third conditional (if + had done, would have done)
   - EN: An imagined past: *if* + *had* + 3rd form, not *would have*.
   - LV: Iedomāta pagātne: *if + had* + 3. forma, nevis *would have*.
16. *If she hadn't checked the code, the app ___ . (crash)* → **would have crashed | 'd have crashed | might have crashed | could have crashed** · catches: *would crash* · Third conditional (if + had done, would have done)
   - EN: The result in an imagined past: *would have* + 3rd form.
   - LV: *Sekas* iedomātā pagātnē: *would have* + 3. forma.
17. *The doctor told me ___ anything heavy for a week. (not / lift)* → **not to lift** · catches: *don't lift* · Reported questions and requests
   - EN: A reported instruction: *told me not to* + base form.
   - LV: Atstāstīts norādījums: *told me not to* + pamatforma.
18. *She apologised ___ the meeting without a word. (leave)* → **for leaving | for having left** · catches: *to be* · Reporting verbs (suggest, admit, promise...)
   - EN: *apologise for* + *-ing* (or *having* + 3rd form).
   - LV: *apologise for* + darbības vārds ar *-ing* (vai *having* + 3. forma).
19. *Our guide suggested ___ the museum early to avoid the queues. (visit)* → **visiting | that we visit | we visit | that we should visit | we should visit | that we visited | we visited** · catches: *to take* · Reporting verbs (suggest, admit, promise...)
   - EN: *suggest* + *-ing* (or *suggest that we visit / should visit*), never *suggest to*.
   - LV: *suggest* + darbības vārds ar *-ing* (vai *suggest that we visit / should visit*), nekad *suggest to*.
20. *I'm not sure what time the meeting ___ . (start)* → **starts | will start | is going to start | is starting** · catches: *does start* · Indirect questions, more uses (I don't know what to do)
   - EN: Inside a sentence the question has statement order, without *does*.
   - LV: Teikuma iekšienē jautājumam ir *apgalvojuma* secība, bez *does*.
21. *She was smiling when I saw her, but her eyes were red because she ___ . (cry)* → **had been crying | 'd been crying | had cried | 'd cried** · catches: *was crying* · Narrative tenses with past perfect continuous
   - EN: The crying was over before that moment and we see its result: *had been* + *-ing* (*had cried* is also possible).
   - LV: Raudāšana bija beigusies pirms tā brīža, un mēs redzam tās rezultātu: *had been* + *-ing* (iespējams arī *had cried*).
22. *If I had learned to drive at eighteen, I ___ so much on taxis now. (not / spend)* → **wouldn't spend | would not spend | wouldn't be spending | would not be spending | might not spend** · catches: *would have been* · Mixed conditionals
   - EN: A past event with a result now: *would* + base form, because of *now*.
   - LV: Pagātnes notikums ar sekām tagad: *would* + pamatforma, jo ir *now*.
23. *If I ___ allergic to cats, I would have stayed at Anna's place last weekend. (not / be)* → **weren't | wasn't | were not | was not | hadn't been | had not been** · catches: *hadn't been* · Mixed conditionals
   - EN: A lasting fact now (*allergic*) with a result in the past: *if* + past (*weren't*). *hadn't been* is also correct if you think only of last weekend.
   - LV: Pastāvīgs fakts tagad (*allergic*) ar sekām pagātnē: *if* + pagātne (*weren't*). Pareizi arī *hadn't been*, ja domā tikai par pagājušo nedēļas nogali.
24. *Sales fell sharply; ___ , the company cut its prices.* → **therefore | as a result | consequently | thus | as a consequence | accordingly | hence | for this reason | in response** · catches: *because* · Contrast, purpose, reason and result (whereas, in case, therefore)
   - EN: A result as a new step after a semicolon: *therefore*, *as a result*.
   - LV: Rezultāts kā jauns *solis* aiz *semikola*: *therefore, as a result*.

## C1.1

25. *If she ___ the job in London, she would be living there now. (accept)* → **had accepted | 'd accepted** · catches: *accepted* · Mixed conditionals
   - EN: A past event with a result now: *if* + *had* + 3rd form.
   - LV: Pagātnes notikums ar sekām tagad: *if + had* + 3. forma.
26. *I wish I ___ closer to my office; the commute takes hours. (live)* → **lived | could live | was living | were living** · catches: *would have* · Advanced conditionals: inversion, unless, wish
   - EN: A wish about now: *wish* + past (*could live* is also correct).
   - LV: Vēlēšanās par tagadni: *wish* + pagātne (pareizi arī *could live*).
27. *I wish I ___ that comment in the meeting yesterday. (not / make)* → **hadn't made | had not made** · catches: *didn't make* · Advanced conditionals: inversion, unless, wish
   - EN: Regret about the past: *wish* + *had* + 3rd form.
   - LV: Nožēla par pagātni: *wish + had* + 3. forma.
28. *We'll start at nine unless the speaker ___ late. (be)* → **is | 's** · catches: *isn't* · Advanced conditionals: inversion, unless, wish
   - EN: *unless* already means *if … not*: no second *not*.
   - LV: *unless* jau nozīmē *if* … *not*: otru noliegumu *neliek*. *Tulkojot burtiski* "ja *vien* nekavēsies", iznāk divi *noliegumi*.
29. *___ we left ten minutes earlier, we would have caught the ferry. (have)* → **Had** · catches: *If* · Advanced conditionals: inversion, unless, wish
   - EN: Formal, without *if*: *Had we left* = *If we had left*.
   - LV: Formāli, bez *if*: *Had we left* = *If we had left*.
30. *It's high time the government ___ something about housing. (do)* → **did | was doing** · catches: *does* · Unreal time and preferences (it's high time, I'd rather)
   - EN: *It's (high) time* + subject + past: it should already happen.
   - LV: *It's* (*high*) *time* + teikuma priekšmets + pagātne: tam jau vajadzētu *notikt*.
31. *In future, I'd rather you ___ my phone without asking. (not / use)* → **didn't use | did not use** · catches: *don't use* · Unreal time and preferences (it's high time, I'd rather)
   - EN: *I'd rather* + another person + past: what I want someone else not to do. *In future* shows it is about from now on.
   - LV: *I'd rather* + cita persona + pagātne: ko es gribu, lai cits nedara. *In future* rāda, ka runa ir par turpmāko laiku.
32. *My father insisted ___ us to the airport. (drive)* → **on driving | that he drive | that he should drive | he should drive | that he would drive | he would drive | that he'd drive | he'd drive** · catches: *to pay* · Reporting verbs (suggest, admit, promise...)
   - EN: *insist on* + *-ing* (or *insist that he drive / should drive*).
   - LV: *insist on* + darbības vārds ar *-ing* (vai *insist that he drive / should drive*).
33. *We couldn't decide where ___ on holiday. (go)* → **to go | we should go | we could go | we would go | we'd go | we were going to go** · catches: *should we go* · Indirect questions, more uses (I don't know what to do)
   - EN: Not knowing what to do: question word + *to* + base form.
   - LV: *Nezinot*, ko darīt: jautājamais vārds + to + pamatforma. *Tulkojot burtiski* "kur *mums braukt*", iznāk jautājuma secība.

## C1.2

34. *___ I in your position, I would ask for a pay rise. (be)* → **Were** · catches: *Was* · Advanced conditionals: inversion, unless, wish
   - EN: Formal, without *if*: *Were I* (not *Was I*).
   - LV: Formāli, bez *if*: *Were I* (nevis *Was I*).
35. *If it ___ for your advice, I would never have applied. (not / be)* → **hadn't been | had not been | weren't | were not | wasn't | was not** · catches: *wasn't* · Advanced conditionals: inversion, unless, wish
   - EN: *If it hadn't been for* + noun = without it, in the past.
   - LV: *If it hadn't been for* + lietvārds = bez tā, pagātnē.
36. *The minister denied ___ the report before it was published. (see)* → **having seen | seeing | that he had seen | he had seen | that he'd seen | he'd seen | that he saw | he saw** · catches: *to see* · Reporting verbs (suggest, admit, promise...)
   - EN: *deny* + *-ing* (or *having* + 3rd form).
   - LV: *deny + -ing* (vai *having* + 3. forma).
37. *They accused the company ___ the data of its users. (sell)* → **of selling | of having sold** · catches: *for selling* · Reporting verbs (suggest, admit, promise...)
   - EN: *accuse someone of* + *-ing*.
   - LV: *accuse someone of + -ing*. *Tulkojot burtiski* "apsūdzēja par", *gribas for*.
38. *___ wins the competition will get a trip to Lisbon. (who)* → **Whoever | Anyone who | Anybody who | The person who | Everyone who** · catches: *Who* · Indirect questions, more uses (I don't know what to do)
   - EN: *Whoever* = it does not matter who: the person who.
   - LV: *Whoever* = nav svarīgi, kurš: tas, kurš.
39. *Our holiday plans depend on ___ my boss gives me the week off.* → **whether** · catches: *if* · Indirect questions, more uses (I don't know what to do)
   - EN: After a preposition use *whether*, not *if*.
   - LV: Aiz prievārda lieto *whether*, nevis *if*.
40. *It's about time you ___ to the dentist. (go)* → **went** · catches: *go* · Unreal time and preferences (it's high time, I'd rather)
   - EN: *It's about time* + subject + past.
   - LV: *It's about time* + teikuma priekšmets + pagātne.
41. *If only I ___ my passport at home! (not / leave)* → **hadn't left | had not left** · catches: *didn't leave* · Advanced conditionals: inversion, unless, wish
   - EN: Regret about the past: *If only* + *had* + 3rd form.
   - LV: Nožēla par pagātni: *If only + had* + 3. forma.
