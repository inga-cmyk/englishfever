## r16c-001 · A2.2 · gap · built-in

Task: Hurry up! If you miss the bus, you ___ ___ late.
word_cards: are | being | would | be | will
Key: {"kind": "gap", "accepted": [["will"], ["be"]]}
- reader 1: instruction: No explicit instruction line (only the sentence); acceptable if the UI says to drag cards. Would be (2nd conditional) does not fit after present simple miss, so only one answer; fine.
- reader 2: instruction: No instruction line at all, only the sentence; the learner is not told what to do (drag a card into each gap). "Hurry up!" is a natural cue, not superfluous.
- reader 2: sense: Distractor "would be" is wrong only because of the real-condition "miss" (a few learners/natives might accept it loosely as less natural, not correct). "are being" and "being be" style combinations are clearly wrong.
