# Unit 1 Worksheet – Name: ____________  Date: ________

**A. Circle the correct form.**
1. I (go / am going) to work by bus every day.
2. Look! It (rains / is raining).
3. She (has lived / lived) here since 2018.
4. We (have visited / visited) Rome last year.

**B. Complete with the verb in brackets.**
1. They ______________ (watch) a film at the moment.
2. I ______________ (never / try) sushi.
3. He ______________ (move) to Riga in 2019.
4. How long ______ you ______ (know) her?

**C. Mistake hunt.** Correct the error.
1. I have seen him yesterday. → ______________________
2. She is knowing the answer. → ______________________

**D. Write.** 6 sentences about yourself (2 present simple, 1 present continuous, 2 present perfect, 1 past simple).

_Answers:_ A: go, is raining, has lived, visited · B: are watching, have never tried, moved, have/known · C: I saw him yesterday; She knows the answer.
