Smart homes: are they a good idea?

A smart home uses internet-connected devices to control things like heating, lights and security cameras. You can use an app on your phone, or you can simply talk to the system. Today about 15% of homes in Europe have at least one smart device, and experts predict that the number will double by 2028.

The advantages. Smart homes save energy. For example, a smart thermostat learns when you are at home and turns the heating off when you leave, so your bills will be lower. Smart locks are also convenient: you won't need keys anymore, and you can open the door for a friend while you're at work. For older people, smart devices can be very useful, because they can remind them to take medicine or call for help.

The disadvantages. The first problem is the price. A full system can cost thousands of euros. Second, devices need a good internet connection; if it stops, your home may not work properly. Finally, there are worries about privacy. Many devices collect information about us, and hackers can sometimes break into them. Experts advise people to choose a strong password for each device and update the software regularly.
