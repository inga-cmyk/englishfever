🎯 What to do

Write one email (100–120 words). Choose Option A or Option B. You can write in the text box or upload a file. You may write the second email for extra practice and send it as a second file.

✍️ Choose your task

✉️ Option A: informal email

Write to a friend. Tell them about your routine, a new hobby you have started, and what you did last weekend. Invite them to do something together.

✉️ Option B: formal email

Write to a language school. Ask about evening English classes. Say who you are, what you do in the week, and ask about the price and the start date.
