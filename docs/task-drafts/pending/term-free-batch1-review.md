# Batch 1 without grammar terms: 91 tasks (draft, 6 November)

The instruction no longer names the form. The context (a time word, a situation, a frame, a start) makes the form necessary.
Where a second form is also correct, it is accepted, and the feedback for **that** answer explains what it means (*note for this answer*).
Answers stay as now unless a line says otherwise. Nothing here is in the app until the teacher approves.

## 4. "Complete the question tag" / "Add a question tag" (14, b1-new-3)

- Gap tasks (8): **Complete the short question at the end:** *Your sister can drive, ___ she?* (answers as now)
- Sentence tasks (6): **Add a short question at the end to check you are right:** *You like tea.* → *You like tea, don't you?*

## 5. "Complete with the correct preposition" / "Write the preposition" (12, a2-mistakes-1, more-1)

New instruction: **Write the missing word:** (each sentence has only one word that fits: *listen ___ music*, *wait ___ the taxi*, *depends ___ the weather* …)

## 6. "Rewrite as a second / third / mixed conditional" (9: 3 built-in, 6 prepared)

- Second: **Imagine it is different. Start with *If I* (*If he*):** *I don't have enough money, so I can't buy a new laptop.*
- Third: **Imagine it happened differently. Start with *If I* (*If she*, *If he*):** *I didn't know, so I didn't help.*
- Mixed (cm-B22-3a-r2): **Imagine the past was different, and say how things would be now. Start with *If he*:** *He didn't take the job, so he isn't living in Paris now.*
- Answers as now (both clause orders stay accepted where they were).

## 7. "Complete with a suitable modal verb" / "with a modal verb" (8, b1-new-4, b1-cond-rep-1)

New instruction: **Write the missing word or words:** (answers as now; they already accept every form that fits, e.g. *might / may / could / will* for *It ___ rain later*).
b1-cond-rep-1 203: **Report the question:** *'Can you help me?' She asked if I ___ help her.* → could / would.

## 8. "Write the plural" (6, a2-mistakes-1, more-1)

New instruction: **Complete:** *One woman, two ___.* (the frame *one … two* does the work)

## 9. "Complete with an adverb" / "Rewrite with an adverb" (7, b1-new-5)

- 61–64: **Complete the second sentence so that it means the same:** *He is a fast driver. He drives ___.*
- 65: **Write one word that fits:** *I ___ drink coffee in the evening because it keeps me awake.* (never / rarely / hardly ever / seldom)
- 66: **Write one word that fits:** *This coffee is ___ hot to drink. I can't drink it.* (too / much too / far too / way too)
- 80: **Say the same with *drives*:** *She is a careful driver.* → *She drives carefully.*

## What the app needs for the notes

A new column *answer notes* in the task file: for an accepted answer, the text shown instead of the general explanation, in English and Latvian.
The grader shows it only when the learner wrote that answer. Built-in tasks get the same notes through their corrected version.
