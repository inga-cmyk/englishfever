Same rule (teacher, 8 November): the wrong chips are **real forms or words of the same kind** that do not fit; no forms that do not exist (*wills, coulds, start does, had went*) and no word of another kind (*bore, beauty, harden, rarity, extremity*).

Every new chip was checked: no other combination gives a correct sentence. ## Stay as they are

- a2-mistakes-1 (prepositions, agreement, plurals: *womans, childs, tooths, mouses*): the A2 topic is exactly this form; the wrong forms are the typical mistakes
- prepositions, articles, quantity, relative words, time words, question tags, word order in reported and indirect questions: all options are real words that do not fit
- *used to* (*Did you use / used*, *isn't used to live*), verb patterns (*to working*, *signing*): real forms or typical mistakes of the topic
- after a helping word (*been able / can*, *might be / being*, *needn't have / has*): only one form fits after the helping word
- two objects (*me / I / my / to me*), adjective order, *so / such*, *someone / anything*, *Everyone is / are*: real words that do not fit
- learning drag tasks (reported verbs, *in spite of*, *so as not to*, *whoever*, *after that*), *Had it not been*, *I'd rather you didn't tell*, *I'd sooner take*, *wish*, *congratulate on*: real forms that do not fit
