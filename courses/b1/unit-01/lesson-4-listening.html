Unit 1 · Listening (exam practice)

Exam tips: 1) Read the questions before you listen. 2) You hear the recording twice. 3) Listen for the answer, not for every word. 4) Write an answer for every question.

Two friends talk about the weekend

Audio: [audio src="/wp-content/uploads/english-fever/b1-unit-01-listening-practice.mp3"]

Listen twice. Then answer the questions.

[h5p slug="b1-unit-1-listening-exam-practice"]
Show the script (read it after you finish)

Tom: Hi Lena! Long time no see. What have you been doing?
Lena: Hi Tom! I'm busy. I've just started a new job, so I commute for an hour every day.
Tom: Wow. Do you like it?
Lena: Yes, I do. But I don't have much time for hobbies now. What about you?
Tom: Well, I took up cycling last month. I go out every Saturday morning with a group of friends.
Lena: That sounds great! How far do you cycle?
Tom: About thirty kilometres. Then we have lunch together. Actually, we're meeting this Saturday at the lake. Why don't you come?
Lena: I'd love to, but I can't. I'm visiting my grandmother in Liepāja.
Tom: What about Sunday, then? We could go to the cinema.
Lena: Sunday is perfect! There's a new comedy at the Splendid Palace. Let's go at six o'clock.
Tom: Great. I'll buy the tickets online. See you on Sunday!
