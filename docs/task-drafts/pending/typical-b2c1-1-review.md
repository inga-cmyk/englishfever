# Typed forms for B2 and C1: typical mistakes (41), draft

For the level test (type the form, nothing to guess) and for practice. Each task has one gap; the word in brackets is the base. *catches* = the typical wrong answer the task is there to find. Not in the app until the teacher approves.


## B2.1

1. *If I ___ her number, I would call her right now. (know)* → **knew** · catches: *would know* · Second conditional (if + past, would)
   - EN: An imagined present: *if* + past (*knew*), not *would*.
   - LV: Iedomāta tagadne: *if* + pagātne (*knew*), nevis *would*. *Tulkojot burtiski* "ja es zinātu", *gribas would*, bet aiz *if* tā nav.
2. *If the train ___ faster, more people would use it for work. (be)* → **was | were** · catches: *would be* · Second conditional (if + past, would)
   - EN: An imagined present: *if* + past (*knew*), not *would*.
   - LV: Iedomāta tagadne: *if* + pagātne (*knew*), nevis *would*. *Tulkojot burtiski* "ja es zinātu", *gribas would*, bet aiz *if* tā nav.
3. *I ___ more if the gym were closer to my office. (exercise)* → **would exercise | 'd exercise** · catches: *will exercise* · Second conditional (if + past, would)
   - EN: The result of an imagined present: *would* + base form.
   - LV: Iedomātas tagadnes *sekas*: *would* + pamatforma.
4. *If we ___ earlier, we wouldn't have missed the flight. (leave)* → **had left | 'd left** · catches: *would have left* · Third conditional (if + had done, would have done)
   - EN: An imagined past: *if* + *had* + 3rd form, not *would have*.
   - LV: Iedomāta pagātne: *if + had* + 3. forma, nevis *would have*.
5. *If you had asked me, I ___ you with the move. (help)* → **would have helped | 'd have helped** · catches: *would helped* · Third conditional (if + had done, would have done)
   - EN: The result in an imagined past: *would have* + 3rd form.
   - LV: *Sekas* iedomātā pagātnē: *would have* + 3. forma.
6. *Tom said he ___ the report the next day. (send)* → **would send | 'd send** · catches: *will send* · Reported statements (tense changes)
   - EN: Reported later: *will* becomes *would*.
   - LV: Atstāstot vēlāk, *will* kļūst par *would*. *Tulkojot burtiski* "teica, ka nosūtīs", *gribas will*.
7. *She told me she ___ in Riga for two years. (live)* → **had lived | had been living | 'd lived | 'd been living** · catches: *has lived* · Reported statements (tense changes)
   - EN: Reported later: *has lived* becomes *had lived*.
   - LV: Atstāstot vēlāk, *has lived* kļūst par *had lived*.
8. *The receptionist asked me where I ___ from. (be)* → **was** · catches: *am* · Reported questions and requests
   - EN: A reported question: statement order and one step back in time: *where I was from*.
   - LV: Atstāstītā jautājumā: *apgalvojuma* secība un laiks soli atpakaļ: *where I was from*.
9. *She asked me how long I ___ English. (study)* → **had been studying | had studied | 'd been studying | 'd studied** · catches: *have been studying* · Reported questions and requests
   - EN: A reported question: one step back in time: *had been studying*.
   - LV: Atstāstītā jautājumā laiks soli atpakaļ: *had been studying*.
10. *Could you tell me what time the museum ___? (open)* → **opens** · catches: *does open* · Indirect questions (Could you tell me where...)
   - EN: After *Could you tell me* the word order is like a statement, without *does*.
   - LV: Aiz *Could you tell me* vārdu secība ir kā apgalvojumā, bez *does*.
11. *When I finally got through, I ___ on hold for forty minutes. (wait)* → **had been waiting | 'd been waiting** · catches: *was waiting* · Narrative tenses with past perfect continuous
   - EN: How long something went on before a moment in the past: *had been* + *-ing*.
   - LV: Cik ilgi kaut kas notika līdz kādam brīdim pagātnē: *had been + -ing*.
12. *___ the rain, we finished the hike. (despite / although)* → **Despite** · catches: *Although* · Contrast, purpose and reason (although, despite, so that)
   - EN: *Despite* + noun (*the rain*); *although* + subject + verb.
   - LV: *Despite* + lietvārds (*the rain*); *although* + teikuma priekšmets + darbības vārds.
13. *She took notes ___ she wouldn't forget anything. (so that / in order to)* → **so that** · catches: *in order to* · Contrast, purpose and reason (although, despite, so that)
   - EN: *so that* + subject + verb; *in order to* + base form.
   - LV: *so that* + teikuma priekšmets + darbības vārds; *in order to* + pamatforma.
14. *Take an umbrella in case it ___ later. (rain)* → **rains** · catches: *will rain* · Contrast, purpose, reason and result (whereas, in case, therefore)
   - EN: After *in case* the present, not *will*.
   - LV: Aiz *in case* tagadne, nevis *will*. *Tulkojot burtiski* "ja *nu* līs", *gribas will*.

## B2.2

15. *I would have gone to the concert if I ___ about it. (know)* → **had known | 'd known** · catches: *would have known* · Third conditional (if + had done, would have done)
   - EN: An imagined past: *if* + *had* + 3rd form, not *would have*.
   - LV: Iedomāta pagātne: *if + had* + 3. forma, nevis *would have*.
16. *If she hadn't checked the code, the app ___ . (crash)* → **would have crashed | 'd have crashed** · catches: *would crash* · Third conditional (if + had done, would have done)
   - EN: The result in an imagined past: *would have* + 3rd form.
   - LV: *Sekas* iedomātā pagātnē: *would have* + 3. forma.
17. *The doctor told me ___ lift anything heavy for a week. (not)* → **not to** · catches: *don't* · Reported questions and requests
   - EN: A reported instruction: *told me not to* + base form.
   - LV: Atstāstīts norādījums: *told me not to* + pamatforma.
18. *He apologised ___ late for the meeting. (be)* → **for being** · catches: *to be* · Reporting verbs (suggest, admit, promise...)
   - EN: *apologise for* + *-ing*.
   - LV: *apologise for + -ing*.
19. *My colleague suggested ___ a taxi to the airport. (take)* → **taking** · catches: *to take* · Reporting verbs (suggest, admit, promise...)
   - EN: *suggest* + *-ing* (or *suggest that we take*), never *suggest to*.
   - LV: *suggest + -ing* (vai *suggest that we take*), nekad *suggest to*. *Tulkojot burtiski "ieteica* paņemt", *gribas to take*.
20. *I'm not sure what time the meeting ___ . (start)* → **starts | will start** · catches: *does start* · Indirect questions, more uses (I don't know what to do)
   - EN: Inside a sentence the question has statement order, without *does*.
   - LV: Teikuma iekšienē jautājumam ir *apgalvojuma* secība, bez *does*.
21. *Her eyes were red because she ___ . (cry)* → **had been crying | 'd been crying** · catches: *was crying* · Narrative tenses with past perfect continuous
   - EN: The result we see, from an activity before: *had been* + *-ing*.
   - LV: *Redzams* rezultāts no agrākas darbības: *had been + -ing*.
22. *If I had studied medicine, I ___ a doctor now. (be)* → **would be | 'd be** · catches: *would have been* · Mixed conditionals
   - EN: A past choice with a result now: *would* + base form, because of *now*.
   - LV: Pagātnes izvēle ar sekām tagad: *would* + pamatforma, jo ir *now*.
23. *If I ___ so shy, I would have spoken to her at the party. (not / be)* → **weren't | wasn't | were not | was not** · catches: *hadn't been* · Mixed conditionals
   - EN: A quality now (*so shy*) with a result in the past: *if* + past.
   - LV: Īpašība tagad (*so shy*) ar sekām pagātnē: *if* + pagātne.
24. *Sales fell sharply; ___ , the company cut its prices. (therefore / because)* → **therefore** · catches: *because* · Contrast, purpose, reason and result (whereas, in case, therefore)
   - EN: A result as a new step after a semicolon: *therefore*.
   - LV: Rezultāts kā jauns *solis* aiz *semikola*: *therefore*.

## C1.1

25. *If she ___ the job in London, she would be living there now. (accept)* → **had accepted | 'd accepted** · catches: *accepted* · Mixed conditionals
   - EN: A past event with a result now: *if* + *had* + 3rd form.
   - LV: Pagātnes notikums ar sekām tagad: *if + had* + 3. forma.
26. *I wish I ___ more time to travel this year. (have)* → **had** · catches: *would have* · Advanced conditionals: inversion, unless, wish
   - EN: A wish about now: *wish* + past.
   - LV: Vēlēšanās par tagadni: *wish* + pagātne. *Tulkojot burtiski* "es gribētu, lai man būtu", *gribas would have*.
27. *I wish I ___ that comment in the meeting yesterday. (not / make)* → **hadn't made | had not made** · catches: *didn't make* · Advanced conditionals: inversion, unless, wish
   - EN: Regret about the past: *wish* + *had* + 3rd form.
   - LV: Nožēla par pagātni: *wish + had* + 3. forma.
28. *We'll start at nine unless the speaker ___ late. (be)* → **is** · catches: *isn't* · Advanced conditionals: inversion, unless, wish
   - EN: *unless* already means *if … not*: no second *not*.
   - LV: *unless* jau nozīmē *if* … *not*: otru noliegumu *neliek*. *Tulkojot burtiski* "ja *vien* nekavēsies", iznāk divi *noliegumi*.
29. *___ I known about the strike, I would have booked another flight. (have)* → **Had** · catches: *If* · Advanced conditionals: inversion, unless, wish
   - EN: Formal, without *if*: *Had I known*.
   - LV: Formāli, bez *if*: *Had I known.*
30. *It's high time the government ___ something about housing. (do)* → **did** · catches: *does* · Unreal time and preferences (it's high time, I'd rather)
   - EN: *It's (high) time* + subject + past: it should already happen.
   - LV: *It's* (*high*) *time* + teikuma priekšmets + pagātne: tam jau vajadzētu *notikt*.
31. *I'd rather you ___ my phone without asking. (not / use)* → **didn't use | did not use** · catches: *don't use* · Unreal time and preferences (it's high time, I'd rather)
   - EN: *I'd rather* + another person + past.
   - LV: *I'd rather* + cits cilvēks + pagātne.
32. *She insisted ___ for the whole dinner. (pay)* → **on paying** · catches: *to pay* · Reporting verbs (suggest, admit, promise...)
   - EN: *insist on* + *-ing*.
   - LV: *insist on + -ing*.
33. *We couldn't decide where ___ on holiday. (go)* → **to go** · catches: *should we go* · Indirect questions, more uses (I don't know what to do)
   - EN: Not knowing what to do: question word + *to* + base form.
   - LV: *Nezinot*, ko darīt: jautājamais vārds + to + pamatforma. *Tulkojot burtiski* "kur *mums braukt*", iznāk jautājuma secība.

## C1.2

34. *___ I in your position, I would ask for a pay rise. (be)* → **Were** · catches: *Was* · Advanced conditionals: inversion, unless, wish
   - EN: Formal, without *if*: *Were I* (not *Was I*).
   - LV: Formāli, bez *if*: *Were I* (nevis *Was I*).
35. *If it ___ for your advice, I would never have applied. (not / be)* → **hadn't been | had not been** · catches: *wasn't* · Advanced conditionals: inversion, unless, wish
   - EN: *If it hadn't been for* + noun = without it, in the past.
   - LV: *If it hadn't been for* + lietvārds = bez tā, pagātnē.
36. *The minister denied ___ the report before it was published. (see)* → **having seen | seeing** · catches: *to see* · Reporting verbs (suggest, admit, promise...)
   - EN: *deny* + *-ing* (or *having* + 3rd form).
   - LV: *deny + -ing* (vai *having* + 3. forma).
37. *They accused the company ___ the data of its users. (sell)* → **of selling** · catches: *for selling* · Reporting verbs (suggest, admit, promise...)
   - EN: *accuse someone of* + *-ing*.
   - LV: *accuse someone of + -ing*. *Tulkojot burtiski* "apsūdzēja par", *gribas for*.
38. *___ wins the competition will get a trip to Lisbon. (who)* → **Whoever** · catches: *Who* · Indirect questions, more uses (I don't know what to do)
   - EN: *Whoever* = it does not matter who: the person who.
   - LV: *Whoever* = nav svarīgi, kurš: tas, kurš.
39. *It depends on ___ the budget is approved. (whether / if)* → **whether** · catches: *if* · Indirect questions, more uses (I don't know what to do)
   - EN: After a preposition use *whether*, not *if*.
   - LV: Aiz prievārda lieto *whether*, nevis *if*.
40. *It's about time you ___ to the dentist. (go)* → **went** · catches: *go* · Unreal time and preferences (it's high time, I'd rather)
   - EN: *It's about time* + subject + past.
   - LV: *It's about time* + teikuma priekšmets + pagātne.
41. *If only I ___ my passport at home! (not / leave)* → **hadn't left | had not left** · catches: *didn't leave* · Advanced conditionals: inversion, unless, wish
   - EN: Regret about the past: *If only* + *had* + 3rd form.
   - LV: Nožēla par pagātni: *If only + had* + 3. forma.
