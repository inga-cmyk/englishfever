# English Fever B1 – Intermediate English

**Mērķauditorija:** latviešu valodas runātāji ar A2 līmeni, kas vēlas sasniegt B1.
**Apjoms:** 8 nodarbības, katra ~60–90 min mācību laika.

| Unit | Tēma / Topic | Galvenā gramatika | Leksika |
|------|--------------|-------------------|---------|
| 01 | Present & past: routines and experiences | Present simple vs continuous; past simple vs present perfect | daily life, free time |
| 02 | Telling stories | Past continuous, past perfect, linkers | narrative words |
| 03 | Plans and predictions | will / going to / present continuous for future | travel, technology |
| 04 | Rules and advice | modals: must, have to, should, might | work and school |
| 05 | Comparing and describing | comparatives, superlatives, (not) as ... as | people, places |
| 06 | If... | zero, first, second conditionals | choices, problems |
| 07 | Reporting what people say | reported speech, question tags | communication |
| 08 | Review and B1 mock test | all of the above | – |

## Each unit contains
- Lesson page (explanation in Latvian, examples in English)
- 3–4 H5P activities (fill in the blanks, drag the words, multiple choice, question set)
- Quiz (10 questions)
- Slides / video script
- Printable worksheet
