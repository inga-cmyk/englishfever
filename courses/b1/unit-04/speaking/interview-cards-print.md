# Unit 4 interview cards – Work and education

**Card 1.** What do you do? Are you a student or do you work?

_Ideas:_ I'm a ... I work as ... / I study ... at ... Add details: how long, what you like.

**Card 2.** Tell me about your job or studies.

_Ideas:_ Present simple for routine, present continuous for now. I have to / I must ...

**Card 3.** What was your favourite subject at school? Why?

_Ideas:_ My favourite subject was ... because the teacher / I was good at it.

**Card 4.** What qualities does a good teacher need?

_Ideas:_ A good teacher should be patient, friendly ... She must explain things clearly.

**Card 5.** Would you like to work for a big company or for yourself? Why?

_Ideas:_ I'd prefer ... because ... On the other hand ...

**Card 6.** What would be your dream job?

_Ideas:_ I'd love to be a ... because ... I'd have to study ... first.

**Card 7.** Do you think it's important to go to university? Why or why not?

_Ideas:_ In my opinion ... You don't have to ... but you should ...

**Card 8.** What rules do you have to follow at work or school?

_Ideas:_ We have to ... We mustn't ... We don't have to ...

**Card 9.** How do you usually prepare for an exam?

_Ideas:_ First I ... then I ... I should/shouldn't leave it until the last minute.

**Card 10.** What advice would you give to someone who is looking for their first job?

_Ideas:_ You should ... You shouldn't ... Don't forget to ...

**Card 11.** Tell me about a time you had to learn something new.

_Ideas:_ When I was ... I had to ... It was difficult but ...

**Card 12.** Do you prefer working alone or in a team? Why?

_Ideas:_ I prefer ... because ... However, it can be ...
