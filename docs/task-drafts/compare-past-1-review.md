## Past simple or past continuous

1. They started playing at 10 and finished at 11.30. At 10.30 they ___ tennis. → **were playing** (played, was playing)
2. I waved to Helen, but she ___. She didn't see me. → **wasn't looking** (wasn't look, weren't looking)
3. Which sentence means 'we had already started dinner'? → **When Karen arrived, we were having dinner.** (When Karen arrived, we had dinner., When Karen arrived, we have dinner.)
4. Which sentence means 'first she arrived, then we had dinner'? → **When Karen arrived, we had dinner.** (When Karen arrived, we were having dinner., When Karen arrived, we have dinner.)
5. Choose the correct option: We ___ each other well. We were good friends. → **knew** (were knowing, was knowing)
6. Use the past simple or the past continuous: We ___ home when I ___ Dan. (walk / meet) → **were walking / met**
7. Use the past simple or the past continuous: It ___ when I ___ up this morning. (rain / get) → **was raining / got**
8. Use the past simple or the past continuous: Matt ___ while we ___ dinner. We were in the middle of it. (phone / have) → **phoned / were having**
9. Use the past simple or the past continuous: I ___ my back while I ___ in the garden. (hurt / work) → **hurt / was working**
10. Use the past simple or the past continuous: I ___ along the road when I ___ Dan. So I stopped, and we talked. (walk / see) → **was walking / saw**
11. Use the past simple or the past continuous: I ___ the party, but Chris ___ to go home. (enjoy / want) → **was enjoying | enjoyed / wanted**
12. Use the past simple or the past continuous: Kate ___ TV a lot when she was ill last year. (watch) → **watched**
13. Use the past simple or the past continuous: We ___ all the way home after the party last night. (walk) → **walked**
14. Word order: *she was reading when i arrived*
15. Word order: *the phone rang while i was having a shower*
