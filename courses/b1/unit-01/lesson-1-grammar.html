🧩 Unit 1 · Grammar: Present and past tenses

🎯 Goal

You will learn to talk about your routines, what is happening now, and your experiences.

📘 1. Present simple and present continuous

TenseUse it forExample
Present simple
I work / she works		habits, routines, factsI study English every evening.
Present continuous
am / is / are + -ing		actions happening now, or around nowI am studying English right now.

⏰ Time words

always, usually, often, every day, never (present simple) · now, at the moment, today, this week (present continuous).

⚠️ Watch out!

Some verbs do not usually go in the continuous form. They are state verbs: know, like, love, want, need, understand, believe. Say I know the answer, not I am knowing.

📘 2. Past simple and present perfect

TenseUse it forExample
Past simple
worked / went		a finished action at a clear time in the pastI visited London in 2022.
Present perfect
have / has + past participle		experience, or a past action that matters now. The time is not important.I have visited London twice.

for or since? Use for + a time period (for three years). Use since + a starting point (since 2020).

Irregular verbs to remember: be – was/were – been · do – did – done · go – went – gone · see – saw – seen · eat – ate – eaten · write – wrote – written · take – took – taken

⏰ Time words

yesterday, last week, in 2022, two days ago (past simple) · ever, never, already, yet, just, so far, since, for (present perfect).

⚠️ Common mistake

I have seen him yesterday. ✗ → I saw him yesterday. ✓ When you say exactly when, use the past simple.

🗣️ Speaking practice

  1. What do you usually do on a normal weekday morning?
  2. What are you doing at the moment? What else are you doing this week?
  3. How often do you meet your friends, and where do you usually go?
  4. What are you learning or trying these days that is new for you?
  5. What did you do last weekend? Give three details.
  6. Tell me about the last trip you took. When did you go, and who did you go with?
  7. Have you ever tried a new hobby? What was it, and how long have you done it?
  8. How long have you lived in your town, and how long have you worked or studied in your job or school?
  9. What have you done this year that you are proud of?
  10. What do you usually do in the evening, and what are you doing differently this week?
