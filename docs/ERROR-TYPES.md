# Error types (draft for the teacher, 25 October)

Background list for the personal plans, Second Chance and the placement test. **Learners never see these names**: the system uses them to choose the next task. Each type: the mistake, the correct form, why Latvian speakers make it, level, the grammar reference (Murphy, *English Grammar in Use*, 5th ed., unit), and the app topics that already have tasks for it.

Status: draft. The teacher adds, removes and reorders; nothing is programmed until the list is approved.

## B. Choosing the tense (one Latvian form, several English forms)

| Code | Mistake → correct | Why (Latvian) | Level | Murphy | App topics |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NOW-HABIT | *Look! It rains.* → *It's raining.* · *I'm going to the gym every day.* → *I go…* | *Es lasu* der gan ieradumam, gan tam, kas notiek tagad. | A2 | 3 | Present simple, Present continuous |
| STATE-ING | *I'm knowing · I'm wanting* → *I know · I want* | Pārlabojums: skolēns lieto *-ing* visur. | A2–B1 | 4 | Present continuous |
| DURATION | *I live here for 10 years. · I know him since 2015.* → *I've lived here for 10 years. · I've known him since 2015.* | Latviski ilgumu līdz šim brīdim izsaka ar tagadni: *dzīvoju šeit 10 gadus, pazīstu viņu kopš 2015. gada*. | B1 | 11, 12 | Present perfect, Present perfect continuous |
| FINISHED-TIME | *I have seen him yesterday.* → *I saw him yesterday.* | Latviski salikto formu (*esmu redzējis*) var lietot ar laika vārdu. | B1 | 13, 14 | Present perfect, Past simple |
| BACKGROUND | *When you called, I watched TV.* (bija procesā) → *I was watching TV.* | *Skatījos* der abiem. | A2–B1 | 6 | Past continuous |
| TIME-CLAUSE | *When I will come home, I'll call you.* → *When I come home…* | Latviski laika palīgteikumā ir nākotne: *kad atnākšu mājās*. | A2–B1 | 25 | Time clauses, First conditional, Future |
| FUTURE | *I call you tomorrow.* (solījums) → *I'll call you tomorrow.* | Latviski nākotnei bieži lieto tagadni: *rīt piezvanu*. | A2 | 19–21 | Future |
| BACKSHIFT | *She said she is tired.* (vairs nav) → *She said she was tired.* | Latviski laiks nemainās: *viņa teica, ka ir nogurusi*. Ja fakts joprojām ir patiess, maiņa nav obligāta (Murphy 47–48), tāpēc uzdevumā konteksts jānosaka skaidri. | B1 | 47, 48 | Reported statements |
| IF-WOULD | *If I would have time, I would help.* → *If I had time, I would help.* | Latviski abās daļās ir vēlējuma izteiksme: *ja man būtu laiks, es palīdzētu*. | B1 | 38–40 | Second conditional, Third conditional |

## C. Sentence structure

| Code | Mistake → correct | Why (Latvian) | Level | Murphy | App topics |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| SAY-TELL | *He said me.* → *He told me. / He said to me.* | Latviski: *viņš man teica*. | B1 | 47 | Reported statements |

## How the system will recognise a type (for later)
- Each wrong option of a choice task and each specific hint of a typed task gets one code.
- The placement test gets 1–2 tasks per type at the learner's level, so the first plan can start from real mistakes.
- A type goes into personal plans only when it has enough tasks at each support level (about 10–15), so the learner does not meet the same tasks again and again.
