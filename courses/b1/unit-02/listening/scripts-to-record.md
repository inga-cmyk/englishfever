# Unit 2 listening scripts (record these; two speakers = two voices)

## Practice recording → b1-unit-02-listening-practice.mp3

Receptionist: Good morning, Hotel Marina. How can I help you?
Customer: Hello. I'd like to book a room for the weekend, please.
Receptionist: Of course. Which dates would you like?
Customer: From Friday the fourteenth to Sunday the sixteenth. Two nights.
Receptionist: Let me check. Yes, we have a double room available. It has a sea view.
Customer: How much is it per night?
Receptionist: It's eighty-five euros, and breakfast is included.
Customer: That sounds good. Is there parking?
Receptionist: Yes, but it costs eight euros a day.
Customer: No problem. What time can we check in?
Receptionist: From two o'clock in the afternoon. Can I have your name, please?
Customer: It's Peter Kalnins. K-A-L-N-I-N-S.
Receptionist: Thank you, Mr Kalnins. We will send you a confirmation email.

## Graded quiz recording → b1-unit-02-listening-quiz.mp3

Announcer: Attention please. Flight BT 211 to Berlin has a delay of ninety minutes. We are sorry for this problem.
Man: Excuse me, I need to talk to someone. I had a connecting flight in Berlin at six o'clock, but now I'll miss it.
Agent: I understand, sir. Can I see your ticket and passport?
Man: Here you are. I booked this ticket online two weeks ago.
Agent: Thank you. I'm looking at the system. There is another flight to Munich at seven-thirty, and from Munich you can take a train to Berlin.
Man: How long is the journey by train?
Agent: About four hours. Alternatively, you can take a night flight tomorrow morning at six-fifteen, and the airline will pay for a hotel.
Man: Hmm. I have an important meeting tomorrow at ten, so the morning flight is too late. I'll take the Munich option.
Agent: Certainly. I'll print your new boarding pass. Please go to Gate 14. Boarding starts at seven o'clock.
Man: Thank you very much for your help.
Agent: You're welcome. Sorry again for the delay.
