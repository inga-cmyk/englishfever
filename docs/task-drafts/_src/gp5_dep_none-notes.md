## Murphy
Unit 132 (verb + preposition: *discuss* takes no preposition, *discuss the plan*, not *discuss about*).

## The task
1. *Say it another way with *discuss*: We talked about the new timetable at the meeting.* → *We discussed the new timetable at the meeting.* (*in the meeting* is also accepted; a fronted *At the meeting* is handled automatically).
   - What the learner must know: *talk about* has a preposition, but *discuss* takes its object directly.
   - Why it competes: the model sentence gives *about*, and the learner's own *diskutēt par* invites *discussed about*. This is the most frequent mistake listed for the point. It is not *Correct the mistake*: no error is shown, the learner must produce the form.
   - Not a duplicate: the existing *discuss* task is a choice with *the plan*. No *timetable* + *discuss* sentence exists in the bank or the drafts.
