Unit 2 · Vocabulary: Travel and holidays

Goal: learn 16 useful words and phrases about Travel and holidays.

Word list

Word / phraseTypeMeaningExample
bookverbreserve a hotel, flight or ticket beforeWe booked a room online.
check inphrasal verbregister at a hotel or airportYou must check in two hours before the flight.
luggagenounthe bags you take on a tripMy luggage was very heavy.
delaynounwhen something is lateThere was a two-hour delay.
sightseeingnounvisiting interesting places as a touristWe went sightseeing in Rome.
accommodationnouna place to stayThe accommodation was cheap and clean.
souvenirnounsomething you buy to remember a tripI bought a souvenir for my mum.
journeynountravelling from one place to anotherThe journey took six hours.
tournouna trip to see a place with a guideWe took a boat tour.
passportnounan official document to travel abroadDon't forget your passport!
set offphrasal verbstart a journeyWe set off at six o'clock.
get lostphrasenot know where you areWe got lost in the old town.
resortnouna holiday place with hotels and activitiesIt's a popular ski resort.
boardverbget on a plane, boat or trainPassengers can board now.
viewnounwhat you can see from a placeThe room has a beautiful sea view.
package holidaynouna holiday with flights and hotel in one priceWe took a cheap package holiday to Turkey.
