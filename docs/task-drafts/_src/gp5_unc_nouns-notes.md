## Per task: what must the learner know, and why the wrong options compete
1. *I have ___ for you!* → **some good news** (a good news / a good new). *news* is uncountable and keeps its *-s*; *a good news*
   is the Latvian-driven mistake (*laba ziņa*), *a good new* the "singular" of it. *good news* alone would be correct, so it is
   not an option.
2. Bank *I found ___ useful ___ on this website.* → **some / information** (chips a, informations). *a useful information* and
   *some useful informations* are the two classic mistakes; every other pair is wrong too.
3. *Which sentence has a mistake?* → **My boss gave me a useful advice.** The other three are correct and about three other
   things: *furniture is* (singular verb), *much luggage* in a negative, *some information* in a question.
4. Order **can i give you a piece of advice** (the statement *I can give you…* is accepted automatically). The learner must
   build *a piece of advice*, not *an advice* / *a advice piece*.
5. Bank *My ___ ___ still at the airport.* → **luggage / is** (chips luggages, are). Both the no-plural and the singular-verb
   rule are needed; *luggage are*, *luggages is*, *luggages are* are all wrong.
6. *We need ___ for our new office.* → **some furniture** (a furniture / furnitures / some furnitures): the typical mistakes.
7. Typed *Thank you! Your advice about the exam ___ really helpful. (be)* → **is | was | has been | will be**. The task is about
   singular vs plural (*are / were* are the mistake), not the time, so every singular form is accepted; *Thank you!* rules out
   *would be* (a polite request, correct English without it).
8. Order **how many pieces of luggage do you have**: *How many* + *pieces of luggage* (not *How many luggages*,
   not *How much luggages*).
9. *Rewrite with furniture: The chairs and tables in this café are new.* → *The furniture in this café is new.* (plus *All the
   furniture…*, *…is all new*, *The café's furniture is new*, *This café has new furniture*, nine variants). The learner must
   change *are* to *is* and keep *furniture* without *-s*.

## Card line needed (Quantity, Watch out or a form line)
- EN: *advice, information, furniture, news, luggage* have no plural and no *a / an*, and take a singular verb: *some advice, a piece of advice, The news is good.*
- LV: *advice, information, furniture, news, luggage* nav daudzskaitļa un nelieto *a / an*; darbības vārds vienskaitlī: *some advice, a piece of advice, The news is good.* Latviski "padomi, mēbeles, ziņas" ir daudzskaitlī.

Without it the card has only "Uncountable + singular verb" (money, hair), not these nouns or *a piece of*.

- Tasks 4 and 8 are both *piece(s) of* orders; that is deliberate (the only places where order needs this point).
