Unit 7 · Grammar: Reported speech and question tags

Goal: report what people said and check information with question tags.

1. Reported speech

We use reported speech to tell someone what another person said. Usually the verb goes one step back in time ("backshift").

Direct speechReported speech
"I work in a bank."She said she worked in a bank.
"I am tired."He said he was tired.
"I have finished."She said she had finished.
"I went home."He said he had gone home.
"I will call you."She said she would call me.
"I can swim."He said he could swim.

2. Other changes

  • Pronouns change: "I like my job." → She said she liked her job.
  • Time words change: today → that day · tomorrow → the next day · yesterday → the day before · now → then · next week → the following week · ago → before
  • say + no person: She said that ... · tell + person: She told me that ...

3. Reporting questions and orders

  • Questions: no question word order, no do/does/did. "Where do you live?" → He asked me where I lived.
  • Yes/no questions: use if / whether. "Are you tired?" → She asked if I was tired.
  • Orders and requests: tell / ask + person + (not) to + verb. "Open the window." → He told me to open the window.

4. Question tags

A question tag is a short question at the end of a sentence. We use it to check information or to ask for agreement.

SentenceTag
Positivenegative tag: You like coffee, don't you?
Negativepositive tag: She isn't here, is she?

Use the same auxiliary verb as in the sentence: It's cold, isn't it? · They have left, haven't they? · He can swim, can't he? · You went there, didn't you? Special: I am → aren't I? · Let's → shall we? · Imperative → will you?
