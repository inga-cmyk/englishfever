🎧 Unit 2 · Listening (exam practice)

💡 Exam tips

  1. Read the questions and the three options before you listen.
  2. Listen for the main idea, not for every word.
  3. Wrong options may use a word from the recording, but they say something different.
  4. Choose one answer for every question, even if you are not sure.

📜 Script

Show the script (read it after you finish)

Receptionist: Good morning, Hotel Marina. How can I help you?
Customer: Hello. I'd like to book a room for the weekend, please.
Receptionist: Of course. Which dates would you like?
Customer: From Friday the fourteenth to Sunday the sixteenth. Two nights.
Receptionist: Let me check. Yes, we have a double room available. It has a sea view.
Customer: How much is it per night?
Receptionist: It's eighty-five euros, and breakfast is included.
Customer: That sounds good. Is there parking?
Receptionist: Yes, but it costs eight euros a day.
Customer: No problem. What time can we check in?
Receptionist: From two o'clock in the afternoon. Can I have your name, please?
Customer: It's Peter Kalnins. K-A-L-N-I-N-S.
Receptionist: Thank you, Mr Kalnins. We will send you a confirmation email.
