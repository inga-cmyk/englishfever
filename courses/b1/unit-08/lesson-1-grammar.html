📘 New: quantity words for shopping and money

  • Countable (can count: apples, euros, shops): many, a few, fewer. How many shops are there?
  • Uncountable (cannot count: money, time, milk): much, a little, less. How much money do you have?
  • Both: a lot of, some, any, enough, plenty of. I don't have enough money.
  • too + adjective (more than we want): too expensive. enough: cheap enough (adjective + enough), enough money (enough + noun).

📘 Passive voice (a small introduction)

We use the passive when the action is more important than the person who does it: The shop is opened at 9. The bags were made in Italy. Form: be + past participle.

🗣️ Speaking practice

  1. What do you usually buy every week? How much or how many?
  2. How much money do you spend on food each week? How many shops do you visit?
  3. What did you buy last month? Was it worth the money?
  4. What are you going to buy soon? Why?
  5. If you had more money, what would you buy?
  6. Compare two shops or two products you know. Which is cheaper or better?
  7. What should people do before they buy something expensive?
  8. What did the shop assistant say the last time you returned something?
  9. Say three things that are made in your country. Use the passive: Cheese is made in ...
  10. How is something you use every day made, cleaned or sold? Use the passive.
