# Unit 7 interview cards – News, media and the environment

**Card 1.** How do you usually get your news?

_Ideas:_ I usually check ... on my phone / on TV. I read the headlines first. I trust ... because ...

**Card 2.** Do you trust everything you read online? Why or why not?

_Ideas:_ Not everything. Fake news ... I always check the source ... A friend told me that ... but it wasn't true.

**Card 3.** Tell me about a news story you remember well.

_Ideas:_ Last week I read that ... The article said that ... It made me feel ... Use reported speech.

**Card 4.** What did your friend/family say to you the last time you talked? (Report it.)

_Ideas:_ She told me that she was ... He said he would ... He asked me if I ...

**Card 5.** What do you do to protect the environment?

_Ideas:_ I recycle ... I try to reduce ... I always ... I don't use ... any more.

**Card 6.** What are the biggest environmental problems in your country?

_Ideas:_ The biggest problem is ... Many people don't ... The government should ...

**Card 7.** Do you think individuals can really help to stop climate change? Why?

_Ideas:_ In my opinion, yes / no, because ... If everybody did ..., we would ...

**Card 8.** Do you prefer reading a newspaper or reading news online? Why?

_Ideas:_ I prefer ... because it's faster / more reliable. The problem with ... is ...

**Card 9.** Which newspaper, website or programme would you recommend? Why?

_Ideas:_ I recommend ... because it is ... It's got good articles about ...

**Card 10.** How has the way we get information changed in your lifetime?

_Ideas:_ When I was a child, ... Now we can ... It's easier to ... but harder to ...

**Card 11.** Would you like to be a journalist? Why or why not?

_Ideas:_ I'd love to / I wouldn't like to because ... A journalist has to ...

**Card 12.** What do you think the world will be like in 2050 for the environment?

_Ideas:_ I'm worried that ... I hope ... I think it will probably ... unless ...
