# Word-bank wrong options, batch 3: passives and *have something done* (draft for review)

Same rule (teacher, 8 November): the wrong chips build **real forms** that do not fit: the active form (*Pizza eats*, *The toys are making*), another tense (*has been written in 1985*), no bare *-ing*, no agreement-only option (*The cake were made*) and no forms that do not exist (*are wash*).

Every new chip was checked: no other combination gives a correct sentence. Forms that would also be correct are kept out of the bank (column *Why*). **Two mistakes found:** *Coffee grows in Brazil* and *Two windows broke in the storm* are correct English, but the task marked them wrong; these 2 tasks and 2 others without a time word get a new text (old version retired).

**55 tasks change, 16 stay.** Research data: drafts with the same text keep their id (only the options change); the 4 new texts and 2 built-in tasks are new versions.
