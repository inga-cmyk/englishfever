🧩 Unit 2 · Grammar: Past continuous, past perfect and story linkers

🎯 Goal

You will learn to tell a story about the past and say what happened first, what was happening, and what happened next.

📘 1. Past continuous

Form: was / were + verb-ing. I was walking. They were waiting.

Use it for an action that was in progress at a moment in the past, or a background action: At 8 o'clock, I was packing my bag.

Use it with the past simple when a short action interrupts a long action: I was walking to the station when I saw my teacher.

📘 2. Past perfect

Form: had + past participle. I had left. She had never flown.

Use it for an action that happened before another action in the past: When we arrived at the airport, the plane had already left.

💡 Tip

Do not use the continuous with state verbs (I was knowing ✗). Use I knew ✓.

🗣️ Speaking practice

  1. Tell me about a day when something unexpected happened. What were you doing when it happened?
  2. What were you doing at eight o'clock yesterday evening?
  3. What was happening around you the last time you were in a busy place, such as a station or a shop?
  4. Tell a short story about your best or worst trip. Use first, then, after that and finally.
  5. What had you already done before you left home this morning?
  6. Think of a time you arrived late. What had happened before you arrived?
  7. Tell me about a time you were surprised. What had you expected before?
  8. What were you doing when you first met your best friend or a colleague?
  9. Describe a journey that went wrong. Use while, when and because.
  10. Tell me about a day you will never forget. Use at least two linking words, such as as soon as, after, before and while.
