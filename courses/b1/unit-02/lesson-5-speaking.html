🗣️ Unit 2 · Speaking (exam practice)

🗣️ How the speaking exam works

  1. Part 1 – Interview (2–3 minutes). The examiner asks you personal questions about your life and the unit topics. Give answers of 2–4 sentences, not just "yes" or "no".
  2. Part 2 – Long turn (1 minute). You talk alone about a picture or a topic. Do not stop! If you forget a word, explain it in other words (It's a thing you use to ...).
  3. Part 3 – Discussion (3 minutes). You and another student (or the examiner) talk about a question together. Give your opinion, ask for the other opinion, and agree or disagree politely.

Useful phrases

FunctionPhrases
Starting an answerWell, ... / Let me think. / That's an interesting question.
Giving an opinionI think ... / In my opinion, ... / I'd say that ...
Giving a reasonThe reason is ... / That's because ... / This is why ...
Agreeing / disagreeingI agree. / Exactly! / I see your point, but ... / I'm not sure about that.
Asking for helpSorry, could you repeat that? / What I mean is ...

🃏 Part 1: Interview cards

Turn a card. Answer the question in 2–4 sentences. The back of the card gives ideas and useful language. Record yourself and listen.

🗣️ Part 2: Long turn

Describe a trip you remember well. Say where you went, who you were with, what you were doing when something interesting happened, and why you remember it. You have 1 minute.

🗣️ Part 3: Discussion

  • Is it better to travel in your own country or abroad? Why?
  • How can tourism cause problems for a place?
  • How has the internet changed the way people plan holidays?
