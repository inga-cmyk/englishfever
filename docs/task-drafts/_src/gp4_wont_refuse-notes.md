# Batch 4, point `wont_refuse` (B1.2): notes of the writer

Topic: Future: will, going to, present continuous (`future`). Murphy, *English Grammar in Use*, unit 21 (*won't* = refuse: *The car won't start*, *she won't listen*) and unit 36 (*wouldn't* = refused: *he wouldn't speak to me*).

Main decision: *doesn't start / isn't starting / can't open / didn't start* are correct English (a fact, not a refusal), so they are never a wrong option, and no gap is open to them (typed gaps either name the two forms, *Write *won't* or *wouldn't*:*, or are the verb after a given *won't / wouldn't*). The wrong options are form mistakes of this point (*won't to*, *won't -ing*, *willn't*, *won't listens*, *wouldn't* + past, *Why the dog won't come?*) or *won't* next to a fixed past time (*yesterday*, *last night*). *won't* for the future (*It won't rain*) never appears: every sentence is a refusal. *doesn't want to switch on* is not used as an option (natives say it informally); it is the *Tulkojot burtiski…* note of task 1.

## Mix (25, as in the brief)
7 choice, 5 typed gap, 3 whole sentence, 5 word order (1 question), 4 word bank, 1 matching (learning).

## What the learner must know, task by task
Choice
1. *my phone still ___* → *won't switch on*: base verb after *won't*; against *won't to*, *won't switching*, *willn't*. LV: the *negrib ieslēgties* calque.
2. *Yesterday … my bike lock ___* → *wouldn't open*: past refusal; against *wouldn't opened*, *wouldn't to open*, *won't open* (wrong with *Yesterday … was late*).
3. *he ___ to me* → *won't listen*: no *-s* after *won't*; against *won't listens*, *won't to*, *willn't*.
4. Correct: *The key card wouldn't open my hotel room last night.* against *wouldn't opened*, *won't … last night*, *wouldn't to open*.
5. NOT correct: *My sister won't to lend me her car.* The others (window / engine / taxi) are correct; one is *The engine didn't start at first*, to show *didn't start* is fine English.
6. NOT correct: *My landlord wouldn't fixed the heating.* Others: printer / cat / a person, all correct.
7. *Why ___ inside?* → *won't the dog come*: question order; against *the dog won't come* (statement order), *won't … to come*, *won't … comes*.

Typed gap
8. *last night … the wifi ___ work* → *wouldn't*. 9. *Can you help me … The zip ___ close* → *won't* (problem now). 10. *The cash machine ___ give me any money yesterday* → *wouldn't*. (Instruction names both forms because without it *didn't / doesn't / can't* fit; choosing between them by time is the point.)
11. *wouldn't ___ to anyone (speak)* → *speak*: base verb after *wouldn't*, not *spoke*.
12. *My son won't ___ his homework (do)* → *do*: no *-s* after *won't* with *he*.

Whole sentence
13. *refuses to open* → *My computer won't open this file.* (also *the file*). 14. *refused to move … last night* → *My neighbour wouldn't move his car last night.* (also *neighbor*, time phrase first added automatically). 15. *Why*-question from *The baby won't eat her soup.*: *Why won't the baby eat her soup?* plus *Why will the baby not eat…?*, *she*, and the short forms without *her soup*.

Word order (contractions fix the people, so no swapped-person order exists)
16. *my dad won't wear his glasses even when he's driving* (also *even when* first, and *won't even wear … when*). 17. *my car wouldn't start this morning so i worked from home* (also *this morning* first and last). 18. question: *why won't you tell me what happened at work*. 19. *my friend wouldn't let me pay for dinner last night* (also *last night* first). 20. *i've tried two keys but this door still won't open* (the clauses cannot swap: the result would not make sense).

Word bank (wrong chips are real forms or common mistakes of this point; no chip makes *doesn't / didn't*)
21. *Yesterday the hotel lift ___ ___* [wouldn't, won't, move, moved]. 22. *My grandmother … ___ ___ anyone help her* [won't, let, lets, letting]. 23. *Why ___ ___ ___ me?* [won't, she, answer, answers]: *she won't answer* is the word-order mistake. 24. *Yesterday the shop assistant ___ ___ me a refund* [wouldn't, won't, give, gave].

Matching (learning)
25. *My laptop won't / Why won't / Last night the baby wouldn't / I asked twice, but she* + *switch on. / the tap stop dripping? / stop crying. / wouldn't tell me.* Each swap fails on grammar (a subject after *Why won't*, *won't / wouldn't* after *she*) or sense (a laptop crying).

## Card line needed
The future card has only *won't* = refuse. Tasks 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11, 14, 17, 19, 21, 24, 25 use *wouldn't* for a past refusal. Proposed addition to *Watch out* (or to the existing *won't* line):
- EN: *wouldn't* = refused in the past: *The car [[wouldn't]] start this morning.*
- LV: *wouldn't* = atteikšanās pagātnē: *The car [[wouldn't]] start this morning.* (Mašīna šorīt neiedarbojās.)
