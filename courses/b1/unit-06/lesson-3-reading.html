💡 Exam tips

  1. Read the title and look at the text quickly.
  2. Read each question. Then find the part of the text that answers it.
  3. Check all the options (a, b, c). Wrong options often use words from the text, but they say something different.

📄 The Mediterranean diet

Doctors often say that the Mediterranean diet is one of the healthiest in the world. People who live in countries such as Italy, Greece and Spain eat a lot of fresh vegetables, fruit, fish and olive oil. They don't eat much red meat or sweets.

Several studies show that if you follow this diet, you will probably live longer. Research from Harvard University found that people who ate Mediterranean food had a 25% lower risk of heart disease. It can also help with stress, because food rich in vitamins is good for your mood.

Is it expensive? Not necessarily. If you buy seasonal vegetables and cook at home, you can save money. "If I knew how easy it was, I would have started years ago," says Ilze, who changed her diet last year. "I have lost five kilos, and I feel fit and happy."

Of course, no diet will work unless you also take care of your body in other ways. Experts recommend that you do at least 30 minutes of exercise a day, drink enough water and sleep for seven or eight hours. If you have any health problems or allergies, you should talk to your doctor before changing your diet.
