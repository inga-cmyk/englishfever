📄 Two cities: Riga and Tallinn

Riga and Tallinn are two of the most popular cities in the Baltic region, but they are quite different. Riga is bigger: it has about 600,000 people, while Tallinn has about 450,000. Riga is also the largest city in the three Baltic states.

Tallinn's old town is smaller than Riga's, but many visitors think it is more like a fairy tale. It has medieval walls and narrow streets, and it is the best-preserved medieval city in northern Europe. Riga, on the other hand, is famous for having more Art Nouveau buildings than any other city in the world. About a third of its centre is made up of these colourful buildings.

When it comes to prices, Tallinn is slightly more expensive than Riga. A cup of coffee in Tallinn costs about 3.50 euros, while in Riga it costs around 3 euros. Public transport is cheaper in Tallinn: residents don't have to pay at all!

The weather is similar in both cities, but Tallinn is a bit colder in winter. Both cities are very safe, and the people are friendly. "I like Tallinn more because it's cleaner," says Sven, a tourist from Germany. "But Riga is livelier at night, and the food is cheaper!" says his wife, Julia.
