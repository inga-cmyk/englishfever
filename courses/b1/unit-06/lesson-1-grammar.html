🧩 Unit 6 · Grammar: Conditionals: zero, first and second

🎯 Goal

You will learn to talk about facts, real possibilities and imaginary situations, and give advice.

📘 1. Three conditionals

TypeFormUse it forExample
Zeroif + present simple, present simplefacts and things that are always trueIf you heat ice, it melts.
Firstif + present simple, will + verbreal or likely situations in the futureIf you eat well, you will feel better.
Secondif + past simple, would + verbunreal or unlikely situations; adviceIf I had more time, I would cook every day.

📘 2. Things to remember

  • The if part can come first or second. With a comma if first: If it rains, we'll stay home. Without a comma if second: We'll stay home if it rains.
  • In the first conditional, we do not use will after if: If it will rain ✗ → If it rains ✓.
  • In the second conditional, we use were for all persons: If I were you, I would see a doctor.
  • If I were you, I would ... is the most common way to give advice.
  • Instead of will you can use can, may, might, should: If you feel ill, you should rest.
  • unless = if not: You will get ill unless you sleep more.
  • as long as / provided that = only if: You can go out as long as you wear a coat.

💡 Quick test

Is it possible (maybe real)? → first conditional. Is it imaginary or unlikely? → second conditional. Is it always true? → zero conditional.

🗣️ Speaking practice

  1. What happens if you don't sleep enough?
  2. If the weather is nice this weekend, what will you do?
  3. What will you do if you get a day off tomorrow?
  4. If you won a lot of money, what would you do?
  5. If you could live anywhere, where would you live and why?
  6. If you were the manager of your company or school, what would you change?
  7. What would you do if you lost your phone in a foreign city?
  8. What will you do this week, as long as you have time? What won't you do unless it is important?
  9. If you had more free time, what would you do?
  10. Give advice to a friend who is always tired. Start with: If I were you, I would ...
