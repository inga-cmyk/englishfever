🔤 Unit 7 · Vocabulary: News, media and the environment

🎯 Goal

You will learn 16 useful words and phrases about News, media and the environment.

🔤 Word list

Word / phraseTypeMeaningExample
headlinenounthe title of a news storyI read the headlines every morning.
journalistnouna person who writes newsShe works as a journalist.
articlenouna piece of writing in a newspaper or onlineI read an interesting article.
broadcastverb/nounsend a programme on TV or radioThe match was broadcast live.
subscribeverbpay to receive a service regularlyI subscribe to a news channel.
fake newsnounfalse information that looks like newsDon't believe fake news.
environmentnounnature and the world around usWe must protect the environment.
pollutionnoundirty air, water or landPollution is a big problem in cities.
recycleverbuse materials againWe recycle paper and glass.
climate changenounlong-term changes in the Earth's weatherClimate change is a global problem.
global warmingnounthe rise of the Earth's temperatureGlobal warming is melting the ice.
wastenounthings we throw awayWe produce too much waste.
renewableadjectivecan be used again, like sun and windSolar power is renewable energy.
harmverbdamage or hurtPlastic harms sea animals.
reduceverbmake smaller or lessWe should reduce our use of plastic.
endangeredadjectiveat risk of disappearingThe tiger is an endangered animal.

🗣️ Speaking practice

  1. Where do you get your news? Do you read headlines or full articles?
  2. Would you like to be a journalist? Why or why not?
  3. What is the most interesting article you have read lately?
  4. Do you watch any news broadcast? Which one, and why?
  5. Do you subscribe to any newsletter or channel? Why?
  6. How can you recognise fake news?
  7. What do you recycle at home? How do you reduce waste?
  8. What can people do about pollution in cities?
  9. How do you think climate change and global warming will affect your country? Which renewable energy is used?
  10. Which endangered animal or plant would you like to protect? What harm do people cause?
