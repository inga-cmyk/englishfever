Unit 8 · Vocabulary: Money and shopping

Goal: learn 16 useful words and phrases about Money and shopping.

Word list

Word / phraseTypeMeaningExample
affordverbhave enough money to buy somethingI can't afford a new car.
bargainnounsomething good that is cheapThese shoes were a real bargain.
receiptnouna paper that shows you paidKeep the receipt.
refundnounmoney given backI asked for a refund.
discountnouna lower priceThe shop gives a 10% discount.
on salephrasesold at a lower priceThe coats are on sale.
cashnounmoney in notes and coinsDo you pay by cash or by card?
budgetnouna plan for how to spend moneyI have a monthly budget.
saveverbkeep money for laterI save 50 euros a month.
borrowverbtake money for a short time and give it backCan I borrow your pen?
lendverbgive something to someone for a short timeCan you lend me ten euros?
costverb/nounthe price of somethingHow much does it cost?
customernouna person who buys thingsThe shop assistant helped the customer.
queuenoun/verba line of people waitingThere was a long queue at the checkout.
brandnouna name of a product made by a companyMy favourite brand is Nike.
walletnouna small case for money and cardsI lost my wallet.
