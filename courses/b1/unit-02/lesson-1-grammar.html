Unit 2 · Grammar: Past continuous, past perfect and story linkers

Goal: tell a story about the past and say what happened first, what was happening, and what happened next.

1. Past continuous

Form: was / were + verb-ing. I was walking. They were waiting.

Use it for an action that was in progress at a moment in the past, or a background action: At 8 o'clock, I was packing my bag.

Use it with the past simple when a short action interrupts a long action: I was walking to the station when I saw my teacher.

2. Past perfect

Form: had + past participle. I had left. She had never flown.

Use it for an action that happened before another action in the past: When we arrived at the airport, the plane had already left.

Tip: Do not use the continuous with state verbs (I was knowing ✗). Use I knew ✓.
