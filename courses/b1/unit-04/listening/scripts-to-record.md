# Unit 4 listening scripts (record these; two speakers = two voices)

## Practice recording → b1-unit-04-listening-practice.mp3

Interviewer: Good morning, Anna. Please sit down. Thank you for coming.
Anna: Good morning. Thank you for inviting me.
Interviewer: First, can you tell me about your experience?
Anna: Yes. For two years I worked as a sales assistant in a clothes shop. I had to help customers and organise the stock.
Interviewer: Why did you leave that job?
Anna: I wanted more responsibility. I think I should learn new skills, and your company has a good training programme.
Interviewer: I see. Our job is full-time. You would have to work on Saturdays about twice a month. Is that a problem?
Anna: No, that's fine. I don't have to look after anyone at home, so I'm free.
Interviewer: Good. The salary is nine hundred euros a month before tax. When could you start?
Anna: I must give one month's notice to my current boss, so I can start on the first of March.
Interviewer: That's perfect. We will call you by Friday.

## Graded quiz recording → b1-unit-04-listening-quiz.mp3

Welcome to the Open Day at Riverside University. I'm Dr Brown, and I'm going to tell you about the Business degree.
The course lasts three years. In the first year, all students have to study the same subjects: economics, accounting and communication. In the second year, you can choose between marketing, finance and management. In the third year you must write a final project, which is about 10,000 words long.
You don't have to pay for books, because the library gives you free access to all of them. However, you must attend at least 80% of the lessons. If you miss more, you might fail the course.
Every student does a placement in a company for ten weeks in the second year. Many students get a job in that company after graduation. Last year 85% of our graduates found work within three months.
To apply, you should send your documents before the first of May. You need a school certificate, and you should write a short letter about why you want to study here. If you have questions, you can visit the information desk in the main hall.
