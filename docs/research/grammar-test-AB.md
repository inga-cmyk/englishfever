# Gramatikas tests: versija A un B (pirmstests / pēctests)

8 kļūdu tipi × 3 uzdevumi (izvēle · ieraksti · izlabo kļūdu) = 24 punkti. Katrs uzdevums 1 punkts. Abas versijas ir paralēlas: tas pats kļūdu tips, tas pats uzdevuma veids, līdzīga grūtība, citi teikumi. Puse grupas sāk ar A, puse ar B (pretsvarošana).

Instrukcija skolēnam: *Choose or write the correct answer. Do not use a dictionary or a translator.*

---

## Versija A

**1. Choose the correct option.**
1. She ___ to work by bus. (a) go (b) goes (c) going (d) is go
2. ___ you like coffee? (a) Do (b) Are (c) Does (d) —
3. I ___ with you. (a) agree (b) am agree (c) agreeing (d) am agreeing
4. It depends ___ the price. (a) on (b) from (c) of (d) —
5. I ___ here since 2020. (a) have lived (b) live (c) am living (d) lived
6. I ___ the email yesterday. (a) sent (b) have sent (c) have send (d) sending
7. Do you know where ___? (a) the museum is (b) is the museum (c) the museum (d) does the museum
8. I'll call you when I ___ home. (a) get (b) will get (c) getting (d) got

**2. Complete the sentence. Use the word in brackets.**
9. My brother ___ in Riga. (live)
10. She ___ meat. (not / eat)
11. Your answer ___ right. (sound)
12. I'm listening ___ music. (write the missing word)
13. We ___ each other for ten years. (know)
14. They ___ to Liepāja in 2019. (move)
15. What time does the shop open? → Can you tell me ___?
16. As soon as she ___, we will start. (arrive)

**3. Correct the mistake. Write the whole sentence.**
17. He speak three languages.
18. Where you live?
19. I'm agree with your idea.
20. Can you call to me after lunch?
21. I'm married for five years.
22. I have seen him last week.
23. Could you tell me where is the bank?
24. If it will rain, we'll stay at home.

## Versija B

**1. Choose the correct option.**
1. My dad ___ coffee every morning. (a) drinks (b) drink (c) drinking (d) is drink
2. ___ your sister have a car? (a) Does (b) Is (c) Do (d) —
3. We ___ with this plan. (a) disagree (b) are disagree (c) disagreeing (d) are disagreeing
4. She is interested ___ art. (a) in (b) on (c) about (d) —
5. She ___ in this company since 2021. (a) has worked (b) works (c) is working (d) worked
6. We ___ that film last weekend. (a) saw (b) have seen (c) have saw (d) seeing
7. Can you tell me what time ___? (a) the train leaves (b) does the train leave (c) leaves the train (d) the train leave
8. We'll start dinner when Dad ___. (a) comes (b) will come (c) coming (d) came

**2. Complete the sentence. Use the word in brackets.**
9. Our teacher ___ at two schools. (teach)
10. They ___ football. (not / like)
11. This soup ___ great. (taste)
12. Look ___ this photo! (write the missing word)
13. I ___ this car for six years. (have)
14. She ___ university in 2015. (finish)
15. Where is the toilet? → Do you know ___?
16. Before you ___, please close the window. (leave)

**3. Correct the mistake. Write the whole sentence.**
17. She work in a hospital.
18. What time you start work?
19. It's depend on you.
20. He wants to join to the army.
21. We live here since 2018.
22. I have bought this phone two days ago.
23. I don't know what does he want.
24. When I will finish, I'll send it to you.

## Atbilžu atslēga un kļūdu tipi

| Nr. | Kļūdas tips | A | B |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1, 9, 17 | 3S (he/she/it + -s) | b · lives · He speaks three languages. | a · teaches · She works in a hospital. |
| 2, 10, 18 | DO (jautājumi, noliegums) | a · doesn't eat / does not eat · Where do you live? | a · don't like / do not like · What time do you start work? |
| 3, 11, 19 | BE-VERB (*I'm agree*) | a · sounds · I agree with your idea. | a · tastes · It depends on you. |
| 4, 12, 20 | DEP-PREP (prievārds pēc vārda) | a · to · Can you call me after lunch? | a · at · He wants to join the army. |
| 5, 13, 21 | DURATION (cik ilgi līdz šim) | a · have known / 've known · I've been married for five years. | a · have had / 've had · We have lived here since 2018. / We have been living here since 2018. |
| 6, 14, 22 | FINISHED-TIME (pabeigts laiks) | a · moved · I saw him last week. | a · finished · I bought this phone two days ago. |
| 7, 15, 23 | Q-ORDER (jautājums teikumā) | a · what time the shop opens · Could you tell me where the bank is? | a · where the toilet is · I don't know what he wants. |
| 8, 16, 24 | TIME-CLAUSE (bez *will* pēc *when / if*) | a · arrives · If it rains, we'll stay at home. | a · leave · When I finish, I'll send it to you. |

**Vērtēšana:** 1 punkts, ja izlabotajā teikumā ir izlabota kļūda un nav ielikta jauna. Pieturzīmes un lielo burtu neskaita. Pieņem arī saīsinātās un pilnās formas (*doesn't / does not*, *I've / I have*). Katram kļūdu tipam ir 0–3 punkti, tos salīdzina pirms un pēc. Kopā 0–24.

**Datu izvērtēšanas veidne** (viena rinda = viens dalībnieks): pseidonīms · versija (A/B) · laiks (pirms / pēc / pēc 14 dienām) · 3S · DO · BE-VERB · DEP-PREP · DURATION · FINISHED-TIME · Q-ORDER · TIME-CLAUSE · kopā.

**Pirms lietošanas:** izmēģināt ar 3–5 skolēniem (vai abas versijas ir vienlīdz grūtas), un šie teikumi nedrīkst parādīties lietotnes treniņā (citādi tests mēra atmiņu, nevis prasmi).
