# Unit 1 – slides / video script (~12 min)

**Slide 1 – Title.** "Welcome to B1! Today: talking about now, always, and before now." (Sveiki! Šodien runāsim par tagadni, ieradumiem un pieredzi.)

**Slide 2 – Warm-up.** Question: "What do you do every day? What are you doing right now?" Pause 10 s for learners to answer aloud.

**Slide 3 – Present simple.** Show 3 examples with routine words. Say: "Habits and facts."

**Slide 4 – Present continuous.** Show 3 examples. Say: "Happening now." Mention state verbs warning.

**Slide 5 – Quick check.** Two gap sentences on screen; reveal answers.

**Slide 6 – Past simple vs perfect.** Timeline graphic: past simple = a dot on the line (finished time); present perfect = an arrow from the past to now.

**Slide 7 – Common Latvian-speaker mistake.** "I have seen him yesterday" ✗ → "I saw him yesterday" ✓.

**Slide 8 – Vocabulary.** commute, hang out, catch up on, get into, take up – one example each.

**Slide 9 – Your turn.** Point to the H5P activities on the lesson page.

**Slide 10 – Homework.** Write 6 sentences about yourself (see lesson section 5) and download the worksheet.
