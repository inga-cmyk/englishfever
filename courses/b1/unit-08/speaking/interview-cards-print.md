# Unit 8 interview cards – Money and shopping

**Card 1.** How do you usually pay when you shop: cash, card or phone? Why?

_Ideas:_ I usually pay by ... because it's quicker / safer. I carry ... with me.

**Card 2.** Do you enjoy shopping? Why or why not?

_Ideas:_ I love / don't mind it. It's relaxing / stressful. I prefer ... to ...

**Card 3.** What was the last thing you bought? Are you happy with it?

_Ideas:_ Last week I bought ... It cost ... I'm happy because ... Use past simple + present perfect.

**Card 4.** Do you prefer shopping online or in shops? Why?

_Ideas:_ I prefer ... because it's cheaper / quicker / I can see the product. On the other hand ...

**Card 5.** Are you good at saving money? What do you save for?

_Ideas:_ I try to save ... a month. I'm saving up for ... If I save enough, I will ...

**Card 6.** What would you do if you won a million euros?

_Ideas:_ If I won a million euros, I would ... first. Then I would ... (second conditional)

**Card 7.** Have you ever returned something to a shop? What happened?

_Ideas:_ Yes, once I bought ... but it was ... I took it back and they gave me ... I was told that ...

**Card 8.** What is the most expensive thing you have ever bought?

_Ideas:_ The most expensive thing was ... I paid ... I saved for ... months.

**Card 9.** Do you think young people spend too much money? Why?

_Ideas:_ In my opinion ... Many young people ... because ... It depends on ...

**Card 10.** What gift would you buy for your best friend?

_Ideas:_ I would buy ... because he/she likes ... It wouldn't be too expensive.

**Card 11.** Tell me about a shop you like and why.

_Ideas:_ It's called ... It sells ... It's cheaper / better than ... because ...

**Card 12.** How do you think shopping will change in the future?

_Ideas:_ I think shops will ... People are going to ... Robots may ... Online shopping will be ...
