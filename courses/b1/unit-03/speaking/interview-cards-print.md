# Unit 3 interview cards – Technology and the future

**Card 1.** What technology do you use every day?

_Ideas:_ I use my ... to ... every day. I couldn't live without ...

**Card 2.** How often do you use social media? What do you use it for?

_Ideas:_ I check ... twice a day. I mostly use it to keep in touch / watch videos.

**Card 3.** What was your first mobile phone like?

_Ideas:_ My first phone was ... It could only ... Use the past simple.

**Card 4.** Do you think children should have smartphones? Why or why not?

_Ideas:_ In my opinion ... because ... On the other hand ...

**Card 5.** What are you going to do this weekend?

_Ideas:_ Use going to for plans and the present continuous for arrangements: I'm meeting ... / I'm going to ...

**Card 6.** What do you think life will be like in 2050?

_Ideas:_ I think ... will ... I'm sure that ... probably ... Use will for predictions.

**Card 7.** Would you like to live in a smart home? Why?

_Ideas:_ I'd love to / I wouldn't like to because ... It would be ... Use advantages and disadvantages.

**Card 8.** How can technology help people learn English?

_Ideas:_ apps, videos, online lessons, translation. For example ... It's useful because ...

**Card 9.** Tell me about a time when technology didn't work well.

_Ideas:_ Past simple + past continuous: I was ... when my ... stopped working.

**Card 10.** What new technology would you like to be invented?

_Ideas:_ I wish someone would invent ... It would help people to ...

**Card 11.** Do you prefer to shop online or in a shop? Why?

_Ideas:_ I prefer ... because it's cheaper / faster / I can try things. The problem is ...

**Card 12.** What are your plans for the next year?

_Ideas:_ I'm going to ... I'd like to ... I hope to ... I will probably ...
