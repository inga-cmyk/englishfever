Unit 1 · Grammar: Present and past tenses

Goal: talk about your routines, what is happening now, and your experiences.

1. Present simple and present continuous

TenseUse it forExample
Present simple
I work / she works		habits, routines, factsI study English every evening.
Present continuous
am / is / are + -ing		actions happening now, or around nowI am studying English right now.

Time words: always, usually, often, every day, never (present simple) · now, at the moment, today, this week (present continuous).

Watch out! Some verbs do not usually go in the continuous form. They are state verbs: know, like, love, want, need, understand, believe. Say I know the answer, not I am knowing.

2. Past simple and present perfect

TenseUse it forExample
Past simple
worked / went		a finished action at a clear time in the pastI visited London in 2022.
Present perfect
have / has + past participle		experience, or a past action that matters now. The time is not important.I have visited London twice.

Time words: yesterday, last week, in 2022, two days ago (past simple) · ever, never, already, yet, just, so far, since, for (present perfect).

Common mistake: I have seen him yesterday. ✗ → I saw him yesterday. ✓ When you say exactly when, use the past simple.

for or since? Use for + a time period (for three years). Use since + a starting point (since 2020).

Irregular verbs to remember: be – was/were – been · do – did – done · go – went – gone · see – saw – seen · eat – ate – eaten · write – wrote – written · take – took – taken
