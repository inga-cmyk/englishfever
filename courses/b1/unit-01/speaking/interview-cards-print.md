# Unit 1 interview cards – Daily life and free time

**Card 1.** What do you usually do after work or school?

_Ideas:_ Use the present simple: I usually ... / After that I ... Add a time word: always, often, sometimes.

**Card 2.** Tell me about your morning routine.

_Ideas:_ First, ... / Then ... / After that ... Say what time you get up.

**Card 3.** What do you do in your free time?

_Ideas:_ I like + -ing. Give a reason: ... because it helps me relax.

**Card 4.** Have you ever tried a new hobby? Tell me about it.

_Ideas:_ Present perfect for experience (I have tried ...), then past simple for details (I started it in ...).

**Card 5.** What did you do last weekend?

_Ideas:_ Use the past simple: I went ... / I met ... / We watched ...

**Card 6.** How do you keep in touch with friends?

_Ideas:_ Phone calls, messages, social media. I usually ... / We often ...

**Card 7.** Do you prefer spending time alone or with other people? Why?

_Ideas:_ I prefer ... because ... / On the other hand ...

**Card 8.** What is your favourite way to relax?

_Ideas:_ Describe it and say when you do it and why you like it.

**Card 9.** How has your life changed in the last five years?

_Ideas:_ Present perfect: I have moved / I have started ... Past simple for finished events.

**Card 10.** What are you doing at the moment in your life? (work, studies, projects)

_Ideas:_ Present continuous: I'm studying ... / I'm working on ...

**Card 11.** Tell me about a person you spend a lot of time with.

_Ideas:_ Say who they are, what you do together, how long you have known them (for/since).

**Card 12.** What would you like to try in the future? Why?

_Ideas:_ I'd like to ... because ... Use 'I've always wanted to ...'
