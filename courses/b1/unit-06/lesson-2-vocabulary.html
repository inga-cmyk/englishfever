🗣️ Speaking practice

  1. What is a healthy diet for you? What do you eat on a typical day?
  2. What is your favourite recipe? What ingredients does it need?
  3. Are you, or is someone you know, vegetarian or allergic to something?
  4. What do you do when you have a headache or a fever?
  5. When did you last get a prescription or have a treatment? What happened?
  6. How long does it take you to recover from a cold?
  7. What do you do to stay fit?
  8. What causes stress for you, and how do you deal with it?
  9. How do you take care of yourself in a busy week?
  10. How big are the portions at your dinner? Do you think they are healthy?
