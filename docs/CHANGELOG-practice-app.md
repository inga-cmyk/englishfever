- **Slip or gap, and locked in (silent, no new messages):** when a learner misses a question in practice, a similar question (same topic, level and thinking level within one step, a different sentence) comes back after one or two others. Right = a slip, nothing else happens. Wrong = a gap: two more questions of that kind follow, from other angles (a different question type where possible). At most a third of a session is spent this way. A fixed mistake is **locked in** when the learner later answers another, different question of that kind correctly on a later day; a wrong answer on that kind of question afterwards opens it again. Second Chance now remembers which mistake each question stood for (one new empty column, `attempts.for_item`; nothing in earlier rows changes), so each right answer fixes exactly that mistake, and a right answer elsewhere fixes the nearest open mistake. The only visible sign is sound (with sound on): a soft questioning two-note *tricky* when a gap is found (instead of the wrong sound), and a fuller *locked in* chord when a mistake is locked in. For you: `…/api.php?r=teacher/evidence` (signed in as a teacher) shows numbers only: probes asked, slips, gaps, how many angle questions were right, and mistakes open / fixed / locked in / reopened across learners. Tests: `tests/probe_test.py`, `tests/comeback_test.py`.
