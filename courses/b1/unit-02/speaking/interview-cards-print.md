# Unit 2 interview cards – Travel and holidays

**Card 1.** Where did you go on your last holiday?

_Ideas:_ Past simple: I went to ... with ... We stayed in ... for ... days.

**Card 2.** What were you doing at this time yesterday?

_Ideas:_ Past continuous: At this time yesterday I was ...-ing.

**Card 3.** Do you prefer to go on a package holiday or travel by yourself? Why?

_Ideas:_ I prefer ... because ... On the other hand ...

**Card 4.** Tell me about a trip that went wrong.

_Ideas:_ Use story words: first, then, suddenly, in the end. Past perfect for what happened earlier: we had missed the train.

**Card 5.** What do you always take with you on holiday?

_Ideas:_ I never travel without ... I also take ... in case ...

**Card 6.** Do you like sightseeing? Why or why not?

_Ideas:_ I enjoy ... because ... I'm not keen on ... because ...

**Card 7.** How do you usually travel to a new place?

_Ideas:_ plane, train, car, coach. It takes ... It's cheaper / more comfortable.

**Card 8.** Describe the best place you have ever visited.

_Ideas:_ Where it is, what you did, what you saw, why it was special. Use the present perfect: it's the best place I've ever been to.

**Card 9.** What would you do if you lost your passport on holiday?

_Ideas:_ Second conditional (see Unit 6): I'd go to the embassy ... Keep it simple: First, I would ...

**Card 10.** Would you rather go to the mountains or to the sea? Why?

_Ideas:_ I'd rather ... because ... I like ... more than ...

**Card 11.** Tell me about a place in Latvia that tourists should visit.

_Ideas:_ It's a ... in ... You can ... It's famous for ...

**Card 12.** Do you think travelling is important? Why?

_Ideas:_ In my opinion, travelling teaches us ... For example, ...
