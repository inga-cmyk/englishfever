📖 Unit 2 · Reading (exam practice)

💡 Exam tips

  1. Read the title and look at the text quickly.
  2. Read each question. Then find the part of the text that answers it.
  3. Check all three options. Wrong options often use words from the text, but they say something different.
  4. Some questions are True / False / Not given. Not given means the text does not say it.

📄 The holiday that went wrong

Last summer, Jānis and his girlfriend Kristīne booked a cheap package holiday to Greece. They had never been to Greece before, and they were very excited. At the airport, however, they heard bad news: their flight had a four-hour delay.

When they finally arrived at their hotel, it was after midnight. The receptionist told them that someone else had taken their room because they hadn't arrived on time. "We were so tired that we almost cried," Kristīne says. In the end, the hotel gave them a different room, but it was next to a noisy bar.

The next morning things were much better. The sun was shining, and they had breakfast on a terrace with a beautiful sea view. They spent the week sightseeing, swimming and eating Greek food. One day, while they were walking in the old town, they got lost. A friendly local woman showed them the way and even invited them for coffee.

"The start was terrible," Jānis says, "but it became our best holiday. We're going back next year, and this time we're going to book a flight with a better airline!"
